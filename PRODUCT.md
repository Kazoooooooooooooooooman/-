# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: university and high-school students in Japan who want their time back from the smartphone — for study, exams, club activities, sport and time with friends. They have tried Screen Time limits and turned them off themselves. They reach the site from Instagram ads, Google search (the column articles) and friends.

Secondary (confirmed, not primary): working adults whose evenings and weekends dissolve into the phone, and parents choosing a phone after a kids' phone. The site's words and pictures are chosen for students first.

## Product Purpose

Soto Phone (ソトフォン) is a phone for Japan with no SNS, games or video. It carries only what you need to go out: calls, SMS, maps, transit, weather, camera, music, disaster alerts, clock, notes, and a focus mode. The name is 外 (soto, "outside"). The motto is 「楽しさは、画面の外に。」 — the fun is outside the screen: people, sport, challenges, nature. It is not a study tool.

The site exists to collect a waitlist before launch. Success is a visitor leaving their email ("完成品を最初に見る") and later joining the prototype trial.

## Positioning

A phone you cannot undo with one tap: the apps that pull you back are not installable, instead of being limited by settings you can switch off. Built for Japanese bands and 技適 certification, with Japanese daily life in mind (transit, disaster alerts, LINE question handled openly).

## Operating Context

- The product is in development. A prototype is being arranged with a factory in Shenzhen; a factory visit is planned for November 2026.
- Path to launch: prototype → a small group uses it for about a week (extendable) → crowdfunding reservation → delivery in signup-number order.
- Signups receive a numbered ticket (No.001…) by email and a short monthly development letter from the founder.
- The landing page runs an A/B test of hero variants (a/b/c/d, plus o/p by URL), with device and dwell logging to a Google Sheet via Apps Script.
- The site has a Japanese/English pair and a column (SEO articles) section with sourced data and credited photographs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed: no SNS, games, video, app store or browser. Screen is normally black-and-white; colour returns when opening a photo. Focus mode stops notifications for a set time and records completed time; parents can see only time, never content.
- Works with the user's existing carrier SIM (planned).
- Price: body planned in the ¥50,000–60,000 range. A ¥3,000 refundable reservation deposit is planned.
- **Undecided — must always be phrased as 準備中 / 予定, never as fact:** Soto's own mobile plan, "the more time outside, the cheaper the phone bill" mechanism, stamps/points from partner shops, LINE support, IC transit payment, launch date, final price.
- Soto Rental (phone storage + rental) exists as a hidden, noindex page and is not being promoted yet.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Soto Phone / SOTO, from 外. Logo: the character 外 inside a phone-shaped frame whose right edge is broken open.
- Motto: 「楽しさは、画面の外に。」 Earlier English line: "Step Out, Turn Off."
- Voice: calm, plain, decisive Japanese. Invite, never push (the founder values 『伝え方が9割』). No obvious filler ("当たり前" statements), no fine print stating the obvious.
- Founder: 佐々木 一真, writing in the first person ("僕").
- The approved product image is the half-real / half-sketch phone (images/hero-phone.*), a design draft, not a product photograph.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product image: images/hero-phone.jpg/.webp (design draft) and a cut-out version for compositing.
- Data used on the site, each with a public source: 165 min/day phone contact time (Hakuhodo Media Environment Institute 2025), 6h44m weekday internet time for high-schoolers (Children and Families Agency FY2025), 23% using 4h+ per day (NTT Docomo Mobile Society Research Institute 2026), 62.5% feel dependent (MMD Research 2024).
- Credited Wikimedia Commons photographs used in the columns (images/column/).
- Absent and never to be fabricated: customer reviews, testimonials, press, partner shop names, user counts, real product photographs.

## Product Principles

1. Never state an undecided thing as a fact; say what is being prepared.
2. Invite rather than pressure: one clear, calm action, no urgency tricks.
3. The point is life outside the screen, not productivity or self-discipline.
4. Show the real thing honestly: design drafts are labelled as drafts, found photographs are credited.
5. Students first: every page should make sense to a 17–22 year old in two seconds.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Signups under 16 need guardian consent; under-13 registrations use a guardian's email. Mobile (Instagram in-app browser) is the main viewing context.
