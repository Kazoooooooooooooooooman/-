How many times a day do you pick up your phone? Studies that actually measured it

November 9, 2026 · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

Could you say how many times a day you pick up your phone? A UK study that logged real use found an average of about 85 times a day. The participants themselves guessed about 37, so the real number was more than double their estimate. Estimated vs. actual

A UK university research team (Andrews et al., 2015) asked 29 university students and staff (aged 18 to 33) who used Android phones to install a logging app, and measured their use for two weeks. Over the same period, participants also estimated their daily use. The analysis covered 23 people.

  • Actual number of uses: an average of 84.68 a day
  • Participants' estimate: an average of 37.20 a day
  • Estimates and actual counts were barely correlated (people who used their phones more did not estimate more)
  • Daily time on the phone averaged 5.05 hours, which was not very different from the estimate (4.12 hours)

People could roughly guess the time, but not the number of times. The researchers concluded that self-estimated numbers of uses "show little reliability for measuring actual uses."

More than half are "quick checks" under 30 seconds

In the same study, 55% of all phone uses lasted less than 30 seconds. Short checks, such as glancing at a notification or the time, push up the daily count. Because they are so short, they may simply not stick in our memory.

The 2019 study (Ellis et al.) collected the "pickups" recorded by iPhone Screen Time from 238 people and found an average of 85.84 a day, almost the same as the small 2015 study.

In the 2023 US report, the median for teens was 51, but the range was huge: from 2 to 498 a day. 11- and 12-year-olds picked up their phones less often, while many of those aged 13 and over did so more than 100 times a day. Even during school hours alone, the median was 13 pickups.

What to keep in mind

  • Studies count "one use" differently. The 2015 study counted each time the phone entered an interactive state, the 2019 study used Screen Time "pickups," and the 2023 report used "pickups" from a logging app
  • Each study involved only a few dozen to about 200 people, and none is nationally representative
  • The 2015 study included only Android users at one university

FAQ

How many times a day is normal to check your phone?

In studies that logged real use, adults averaged about 85 times a day (84.68 among 23 people in 2015, and 85.84 among 238 people in 2019). But individuals vary a lot, so there is no set "normal" number.

How can I find out how often I check my phone?

On iPhone, Screen Time shows your pickups; on Android, Digital Wellbeing shows how often you unlock your phone. As the research shows, our estimates tend to be lower than reality, so it is worth looking at the record once. For tips on using these tools, see if you keep turning off your own Screen Time limits.
