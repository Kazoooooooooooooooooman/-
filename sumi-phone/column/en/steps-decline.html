People in Japan now walk 600 to 800 fewer steps a day than 12 years ago

November 3, 2026 · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

Over the past 12 years, people in Japan have started walking 600 to 800 fewer steps a day. Average steps for adults aged 20 and over fell from 7,233 for men and 6,437 for women in 2011 to 6,628 for men and 5,659 for women in 2023. The 778 steps women lost is about a 7- to 8-minute walk. This summary is based on the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare's National Health and Nutrition Survey.

The short version

  • Steps among adults aged 20 and over fell significantly for both men and women over ten years (Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare)
  • In 2023: 6,628 steps for men, 5,659 for women
  • Among people aged 65 and over: 5,329 steps for men, 4,419 for women

The survey was not carried out in 2020 and 2021 because of COVID-19. The decline has not been a straight line, but the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare sums it up this way: "Looking at the past ten years, steps have decreased significantly for both men and women."

The national target set in Health Japan 21 (third term), the government's health promotion plan, is 7,100 steps a day (age-adjusted). In 2023, neither men nor women reached it.

About the 2024 survey

In the 2024 survey, average steps were 7,071 (7,763 for men, 6,495 for women). However, that year the data was collected and tallied differently from usual (as nationally adjusted figures), and steps were measured with pedometers using three-axis accelerometers. For that reason, it cannot simply be compared with the trend above.

What to keep in mind when reading the numbers

  • The figures were measured by having adults aged 20 and over wear pedometers
  • People with fewer than 100 steps, or 50,000 or more, are excluded
  • The survey does not tell us why steps have declined. Several factors are possible, such as working from home, getting around by car and spending more time at home

FAQ

How many steps a day do people in Japan walk on average?

In the 2023 National Health and Nutrition Survey, adults aged 20 and over walked 6,628 steps a day for men and 5,659 for women.

How many steps a day should you walk?

Japan's national health promotion target (Health Japan 21, third term) is an average of 7,100 steps a day (age-adjusted).

How far are 600 to 800 steps?

With a stride of about 65 cm, 600 steps is about 400 m, and 800 steps is about 500 m. In time, that is about 7 to 8 minutes of slow walking.

Summary

  • From 2011 to 2023, steps in Japan fell by 605 for men and 778 for women
  • In 2023, neither men nor women reached the national target of 7,100 steps
  • Walking just one train stop's distance would make up almost all of the lost steps
