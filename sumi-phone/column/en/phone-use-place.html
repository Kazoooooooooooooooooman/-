Where do people use their smartphones? Time at home, at work and on the move

November 1, 2026 · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

People spend an average of 120.5 minutes a weekday online on smartphones and other mobile devices. Of that, 88.8 minutes, about 74%, is spent at home. Far more time goes on the phone inside the house than on the train. This summary is based on a survey by Japan's Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC).

The short version

  • Mobile internet use is 120.5 minutes on a weekday: 88.8 minutes at home, 18.9 at work and 9.6 on the move
  • For teens, 168.5 of 201.5 minutes (84%) are at home
  • If you want to cut phone time, changing how you use it at home works better than changing how you use it outside

Teens and people in their 20s each spend about 20 minutes on their phones on the move as well. Even so, home is the longest in every age group.

How to cut phone time at home

  • Give the phone a place: when you get home, put it in a set spot by the entrance or in the living room, and don't take it into the bedroom (how to stop using your phone before bed)
  • Set a time with notifications off: divide the day by time, for example turning notifications off after dinner (how to clean up your notifications)
  • Make rules as a family: not just for children; adults follow the same rules (family phone rules)

Summary

  • About 74% of internet use on smartphones happens at home
  • Teens spend about 2 hours 49 minutes a day at home alone
  • To cut back, decide where the phone lives at home and when it is used
