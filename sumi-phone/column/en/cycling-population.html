How many people in Japan go cycling? What national and foundation surveys show

November 9, 2026 · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

8.2% of people in Japan aged 10 and over go cycling. According to the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications' Survey on Time Use and Leisure Activities (2021), that is up 0.3 points from 7.9% five years earlier. While the share of people doing most sports went down, cycling was one of the few that went up. This summary is based on surveys by the ministry and the Sasakawa Sports Foundation.

The short version

  • In 2021, 8.2% of people aged 10 and over had gone cycling in the past year (MIC)
  • Of the main sports, only walking/light exercise and cycling grew over the five years from 2016
  • A survey of adults aged 18 and over shows a fall, from 7.38 million in 2022 to 6.49 million in 2024 (Sasakawa Sports Foundation, preliminary figures)

By type of sport (2021)

Cycling came fourth, after walking/light exercise, training with equipment, and jogging/marathons. The share of people who did any sport at all was 66.5%, down 2.3 points from five years earlier.

The ministry sums it up this way: walking/light exercise and cycling rose from five years earlier, while almost all other sports, starting with bowling and swimming, fell. Both are sports you can do easily outdoors, even on your own. We looked at how the number of joggers and runners has changed in Japan's jogging and running population.

A survey of adults shows a decline

The Sasakawa Sports Foundation's "Survey on Sports Life" reports the share of people aged 18 and over who cycled at least once a year, along with an estimated number of people.

That works out to a fall of about 900,000 people in two years. Both are preliminary figures, however, and the final numbers are given in the full reports.

Things to keep in mind

  • The ministry's survey covers about 190,000 people aged 10 and over across Japan. It asks whether they went cycling in their free time during the year from October 20, 2020 to October 19, 2021. That period included two COVID-19 states of emergency
  • The Sasakawa Sports Foundation survey asked 3,000 people aged 18 and over, with survey staff delivering and later collecting the questionnaires. The age range and the questions differ, so the two sets of numbers cannot be compared directly
  • Both surveys ask about sports done in free time or for leisure. Riding a bicycle to commute, get to school or go shopping is probably not usually included

FAQ

How many people in Japan go cycling?

According to the Sasakawa Sports Foundation survey (preliminary figures), an estimated 6.49 million people aged 18 and over cycled at least once in 2024. In 2022 the figure was 7.38 million.

What happens if you use your phone while cycling in Japan?

Since April 2026, using a phone while riding a bicycle can get you a "blue ticket" (an on-the-spot fine). See a 12,000 yen fine for using a phone while cycling for details.
