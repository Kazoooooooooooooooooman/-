Children's use of generative AI: how many elementary, junior high and high school students in Japan use ChatGPT and similar tools

October 29, 2026 · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

Among elementary, junior high and high school students in Japan who use the internet, 29.5% use generative AI such as ChatGPT, Copilot or Gemini. Among high school students the figure is 46.2%, or nearly one in two. Generative AI was added as a question for the first time in the survey that Japan's Children and Families Agency carried out in fiscal 2025.

The short version

  • Share using generative AI (on any device): 8.6% of elementary, 30.8% of junior high and 46.2% of high school students
  • Share using it on a smartphone: 8.2% of elementary, 27.0% of junior high and 42.8% of high school students
  • Among high school students, girls (54.5%) use it more than boys (38.3%)

Use by school level

The Children and Families Agency's "Survey on Internet Use Among Young People" asks children aged 10 to 17 and their parents. The figures here are the share of children who use the internet on some device (3,030 respondents) who said they use generative AI on that device.

Fewer than 1 in 10 elementary school students use it, but the share rises to 3 in 10 in junior high and close to 5 in 10 in high school.

Compared with other uses

Across all school-age children who use the internet, generative AI (29.5%) is far below watching videos (92.1%), searching and looking things up (86.0%) and games (84.9%), but above e-books (25.1%) and shopping (18.6%). Other uses are covered in what children do online.

The survey also found that 65.2% of children go online for "studying or lessons." It did not ask what they use generative AI for.

Things to talk about as a family

  • Which services they use, and for what (homework, looking things up, chatting, and so on)
  • That answers can be wrong, so anything important should be checked against other sources
  • Not to enter personal information such as their name, address or photos
  • That each service has its own age requirements, so check them before using it
