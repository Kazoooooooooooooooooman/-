How many people listen to the radio? Listening rates and time by age group

November 1, 2026 · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

In the fiscal 2025 survey, 6.0% of people listened to the radio on a weekday, down from 9.2% in fiscal 2020. Meanwhile, 13.7% use services such as radiko (Japan's main app for streaming broadcast radio) to listen over the internet. Some people are taking a fresh look at radio as a medium that doesn't require looking at a screen. This summary is based on a survey by Japan's Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC).

The short version

  • 6.0% of people listened to the radio on a weekday, and 3.7% on a day off
  • Average weekday listening time was 11.4 minutes: 26.1 minutes in their 60s and 19.3 minutes in their 70s
  • 13.7% use services for listening to radio over the internet

These are averages that include people who did not listen. The weekday average across all ages fell from 15.6 minutes in fiscal 2020 to 11.4 minutes.

What's good about radio

  • No screen needed: You can listen while doing housework or walking
  • You can leave it on: You don't have to pick the next video yourself, so your hands stay free for what you're doing
  • Reliable in a disaster: A battery-powered radio keeps the information coming during a power cut or when your phone battery runs out

Summary

  • 6.0% of people listen to the radio on a weekday, and the share is slowly falling
  • People in their 60s listen the longest, at 26.1 minutes on a weekday
  • 13.7% use internet radio such as radiko
