How many people stay at hot springs? Trends in overnight guests and the number of hot spring areas

October 31, 2026 · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

In fiscal 2024 (Japan's fiscal year runs April–March), overnight stays at lodgings in hot spring (onsen) areas across Japan totaled 125.41 million. That is a recovery from fiscal 2020, when COVID-19 brought the figure down to 76.59 million, and almost the same level as fiscal 2019 (126.53 million). This summary is based on the Ministry of the Environment's data on hot spring use.

The short version

  • Total overnight guests at hot spring areas for the year: 125.41 million (fiscal 2024)
  • There are 2,839 hot spring areas and 27,899 hot spring sources
  • There are 7,721 public baths that use hot spring water, and you can visit them on a day trip

Trends in overnight guests

This is a total count of stays, so one person staying two nights counts as two. The Ministry of the Environment treats the number of overnight guests as a "reference figure."

Number of hot spring areas and sources

ItemFY2019FY2024
Hot spring areas2,9712,839
Hot spring sources27,96927,899
Lodging facilities13,05013,425
Public baths using hot spring water7,9817,721

Hot spring areas are counted as places with lodging facilities. The number of hot spring areas is slowly falling, but there are more lodging facilities than in fiscal 2019.

Leave your phone behind and go to a hot spring

  • Go on a day trip: there are 7,721 public baths using hot spring water across Japan. You can bathe without staying the night
  • Keep your phone put away in the changing room: many facilities ban taking photos in the changing room and bathing area. Once your phone is in the locker, it becomes time away from the screen
