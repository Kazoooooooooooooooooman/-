COVID nearly halved the number of people who go to karaoke. Now they are coming back

November 8, 2026 · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

In 2019, 46.5 million people in Japan went to karaoke at least once. In 2020, when COVID spread, that fell to 26.2 million, and in 2021 to 25.9 million. In 2022 it climbed back to 32.4 million. This summary is based on figures from the All-Japan Karaoke Industrialist Association (as published on J-Net21, run by the Organization for Small & Medium Enterprises and Regional Innovation) and on Teikoku Databank's market estimates.

Karaoke participants, 2019–2022

The "participant population" is the number of people who used karaoke at least once in a year. For 2020, an association official told Shukan Josei PRIME that participation was "down 40% from the previous year." Press reports say the figure had hovered around 47 million for the 20 years before COVID (Money Post WEB).

The karaoke box market

According to Teikoku Databank, the karaoke market, centered on Japan's major karaoke box operators, fell from ¥348.5 billion in fiscal 2018 to ¥174 billion in fiscal 2021. It is expected to recover to about ¥320 billion in fiscal 2024. The firm points to the return of year-end and New Year parties and other group events, foreign visitors in cities, and strong demand from families at suburban branches. It also credits "karaoke without singing": renting the soundproof rooms for something other than singing.

Why it is coming back

Karaoke is something you do with friends, family or coworkers in the same room. It fell sharply during COVID, when people met less, and has recovered as chances to get together returned. Part of the market's recovery also comes from "oshi-katsu" (supporting a favorite idol or artist): groups rent a room for hours to watch concert videos or live viewings together (Teikoku Databank). Music concerts, theater and other live entertainment have recovered too (Japan's live entertainment market).

Reading the numbers

  • The participant figures are estimates by the All-Japan Karaoke Industrialist Association. We could not open the association's own website while writing this article, so we used the association's figures as published on J-Net21 (Organization for Small & Medium Enterprises and Regional Innovation)
  • We could not confirm participant figures for 2023 or later on a public page, so they are not included
  • The market size is Teikoku Databank's estimate, based on the sales of companies that run karaoke boxes. It does not include karaoke in bars such as snack bars. The fiscal 2024 figure is a projection as of the end of February 2025
  • The participant figures and the market size come from different organizations using different methods

FAQ

How many people go to karaoke in Japan?

According to the All-Japan Karaoke Industrialist Association's estimate, 32.4 million people went to karaoke at least once in 2022. Before COVID, in 2019, it was 46.5 million.

How big is Japan's karaoke market?

Teikoku Databank estimates that the market, centered on karaoke boxes, will reach about ¥320 billion in fiscal 2024. At its lowest, in fiscal 2021, it was ¥174 billion.

Summary

  • Karaoke participants fell from 46.5 million in 2019 to 25.9 million in 2021
  • In 2022 the figure was 32.4 million and still recovering. The market is expected to return to about ¥320 billion in fiscal 2024
  • Getting together and singing out loud is hard to replace through a screen
