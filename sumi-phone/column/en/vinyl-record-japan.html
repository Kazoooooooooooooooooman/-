Vinyl is back. Japan's record production reached ¥8.44 billion in 2025, the highest in 37 years

November 7, 2026 · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

Most people now listen to music on their phones. Even so, more and more people are buying records, dropping the needle and listening. In 2025, Japan produced 3,378,000 vinyl records, with a production value of ¥8.441 billion. That value is the highest since 1988, 37 years ago. This summary is based on statistics from the Recording Industry Association of Japan (RIAJ).

The short version

  • Vinyl production has grown in both volume and value for five years in a row
  • It fell to about 100,000 records in 2009, but recovered to 3,378,000 in 2025
  • Still, that is less than 2% of the 1976 peak (about 200 million records), so the market remains small

The lowest volume was 102,000 records in 2009, and the lowest value was ¥170 million in 2010. Production then grew little by little, with a big jump in 2021. The 2025 value is about 50 times the 2010 figure.

Compared with the peak years

Vinyl production peaked at 199,752,000 records in 1976 by volume, and at ¥181.238 billion in 1980 by value. After CDs spread, records were barely made at all in the 1990s.

The 3,378,000 records made in 2025 are less than 2% of the 1976 peak. Even if we call it a "comeback", the market is still small. But value topping ¥8 billion is the first time since 1988, 37 years ago.

Japanese music makes up 70%

Of the records produced in 2025, 2,371,000 were Japanese music and 1,008,000 were international music. Japanese music accounts for about 70% of the volume.

Why choose records?

The statistics do not tell us why people buy them. But unlike streaming on a phone, a record takes effort: you take it out of the sleeve, drop the needle, and flip it over when side A ends. It is hard to skip songs, so you end up listening to the whole record. Because it takes effort, some people likely enjoy the time spent listening for its own sake.

Live music and cinemas are also drawing more people in the streaming era (see Japan's live entertainment market (2025)). Things you enjoy with your body and hands, rather than on a screen, may be finding favor again.

Notes on the numbers

  • These are "production" figures, mainly from RIAJ member companies, and are not the same as the number of records sold in stores
  • The values are production values, which differ from retail sales
  • Imported records and used records are not included

FAQ

How many vinyl records are made in Japan today?

In 2025, 3,378,000 records, with a production value of ¥8.441 billion (RIAJ). Both volume and value have grown for five years in a row.

When did records sell the most?

The record highs are 199,752,000 records produced in 1976, and a production value of ¥181.238 billion in 1980.

Summary

  • Japan's vinyl production value reached ¥8.441 billion in 2025, the highest in 37 years
  • 3,378,000 records were produced, about 33 times the 2009 low
  • More people are choosing a slower way of listening that takes a bit of effort
