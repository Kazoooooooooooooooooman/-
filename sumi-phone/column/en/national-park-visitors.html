Japan's national parks had 340.07 million users in 2024, back to 90% of pre-COVID levels

November 14, 2026 · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

In 2024, Japan's national parks had 340.07 million users. After a big drop during COVID in 2020–2021, numbers have recovered to about 90% of the pre-COVID level in 2019 (369.15 million). Counting all "natural parks" together, including national parks, quasi-national parks and prefectural natural parks, the total was 812.62 million. This summary is based on the Ministry of the Environment's Survey of Natural Park Visitors.

Natural park users over time

Japan's natural parks include "national parks" (35) and "quasi-national parks" (57), both designated by the national government, and "prefectural natural parks" (311), designated by prefectures (as of 2024).

Users of all natural parks first passed 1 billion in 1991 and peaked at 1,019.34 million in 1992. Numbers then slowly declined, falling to 807.91 million in 2011, the year of the Great East Japan Earthquake. In 2020, COVID cut users to 62.1% of the year before (554.35 million), and 2021 was even lower at 542.46 million. Recovery has continued since 2022, and 2024 reached 812.62 million, up 3.0% on the year before.

Top 10 national parks by users (2024)

The top park, Fuji-Hakone-Izu National Park, had 124.18 million users, 36.5% of all national park users. The top 10 parks together had 270.94 million, or 79.7% of the total. The Ministry of the Environment notes that the top parks are relatively easy to reach from cities.

Among quasi-national parks, the top three were Biwako (Lake Biwa, 33.99 million), Genkai (28.74 million) and Yatsugatake-Chushin Kogen (14.37 million).

More visitors from overseas, too

As a reference figure, the Ministry of the Environment also estimates how many foreign tourists visit national parks. In 2024 the estimate was 8,485,254, higher than before COVID in 2019 (6,669,131). By park, the most were at Fuji-Hakone-Izu (about 3.89 million), Aso-Kuju (about 1.17 million) and Shikotsu-Toya (about 940,000). No estimates were made for 2020–2022.

Reading the numbers

  • These are user numbers by calendar year (January to December), compiled by the Ministry of the Environment from prefectural reports. Counting methods differ by park and region, so treat them as a rough guide
  • The number of national parks changes over time (Hidaka-Sanmyaku-Erimo-Tokachi National Park was added in June 2024, bringing the total to 35)
  • The foreign visitor numbers are estimates and have a margin of error

FAQ

How many people visit Japan's national parks each year?

In 2024 there were 340.07 million users. Including quasi-national parks and prefectural natural parks, all natural parks together had 812.62 million.

Which national park is the busiest?

Fuji-Hakone-Izu National Park, with 124.18 million users in 2024. Second was Setonaikai (the Seto Inland Sea, 41.61 million) and third was Joshinetsu Kogen (22.1 million).

Summary

  • Japan's national parks had 340.07 million users in 2024, back to about 90% of the pre-COVID level
  • All natural parks together had 812.62 million, about 20% below the 1992 peak
  • Users are concentrated in parks that are easy to reach from cities
