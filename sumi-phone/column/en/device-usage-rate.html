How many people use smartphones, tablets and smartwatches

November 1, 2026 · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

In fiscal 2025, 95.9% of people of all ages in Japan used a smartphone. The figure is above 90% for everyone from their teens to their 60s, and 83.8% even for people in their 70s. One in three people uses a tablet, and one in six uses a smartwatch. This summary is based on a survey by the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC).

The short version

  • Smartphones 95.9%, tablets 33.6%, smartwatches 15.7%, feature phones 10.2%, e-book readers 6.5%
  • Smartwatch use is above 20% for people in their 20s to 40s
  • Tablet and feature phone use fell from the year before

E-book reader use is highest among people in their 40s (11.7%).

How things changed over five years

  • Smartphones: 88.0% (fiscal 2020) → 95.9% (fiscal 2025)
  • Smartwatches: 5.9% → 15.7% (about 2.7 times)
  • Feature phones: 18.1% → 10.2%
  • Tablets: 35.2% → 33.6%

A smartwatch lets you check notifications without taking out your phone. If you want it to save time rather than eat it, it helps to limit which people and apps can send you notifications (see how to tidy up your notifications).

Summary

  • 95.9% of people use a smartphone, and more than 80% even among people in their 70s
  • One in three uses a tablet, and one in six uses a smartwatch
  • Smartwatch use grew about 2.7 times in five years
