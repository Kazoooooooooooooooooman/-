Trust in the media: how much people in Japan trust newspapers, TV, the internet and social media

October 29, 2026 · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

Of newspapers, TV, the internet and magazines, the most trusted was newspapers at 58.8%, followed by TV at 57.9%. The internet came in at 26.3%, less than half the level of newspapers or TV. Here is a summary of trust in the media from a fiscal 2025 survey by Japan's Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC).

The short version

  • "Can trust all or most of it": newspapers 58.8%, TV 57.9%, internet 26.3%, magazines 12.9%
  • For news on domestic politics and the economy, 17.8% trust social media and 12.3% trust video sites
  • The medium people use most to get reliable information is TV (50.3%). But among people in their 20s to 40s, the internet comes out on top

Trust in each medium

The survey asked 1,800 people aged 13 to 79 how much of the information in each medium they think they can trust. The combined share answering "I can trust all of it" or "I can trust most of it" is treated as the trust level.

Compared with the previous year (fiscal 2024), trust in TV (58.2%) and the internet (27.0%) was roughly flat, while newspapers (59.9%) and magazines (15.7%) fell.

Even among teens and people in their 20s, who spend the most time online, trust in the internet is only around 30%. In every age group, TV and newspapers are trusted more.

By news topic: trust in social media and video sites

The survey also asked how much people trust eight kinds of media on different topics (the combined share answering they trust it "very much" or "somewhat"). For domestic politics and the economy, the results were as follows.

Even within the internet, there is a big gap between news sites (47.6%) and social media (17.8%). Compared with 2020, trust in TV fell from 81.4% to 73.4%, and newspapers from 62.6% to 51.9%.

People turn to the internet for speed and for looking things up, and to TV for reliable information. By age group, people in their 20s (51.8%), 30s (51.5%) and 40s (42.6%) use the internet most to get reliable information, while teens and people in their 50s to 70s use TV most.

Summary

  • Trust levels: newspapers 58.8%, TV 57.9%, internet 26.3%, magazines 12.9%
  • On domestic politics and the economy, 17.8% trust social media
  • People use different media for different purposes: the internet for speed, TV for trust
