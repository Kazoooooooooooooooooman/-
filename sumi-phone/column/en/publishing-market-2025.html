Sales of print books and magazines in Japan fell below 1 trillion yen for the first time in 50 years

November 5, 2026 · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

In 2025, sales of print books and magazines in Japan fell below 1 trillion yen. According to the Research Institute for Publications, they came to 964.7 billion yen, the first time this has happened since 1975, 50 years ago. In 1996, the best year on record, sales were about 2.6 trillion yen. The publishing market including digital books was 1.5462 trillion yen, down from the year before for the fourth year in a row.

The short version

  • Print books came to 593.9 billion yen, almost the same as the year before. The decline was in magazines
  • Magazines fell by 10% (41.1 billion yen) in one year
  • Digital grew, but digital manga, which had been driving that growth, lost momentum

The 2025 publishing market, broken down

Print books rose slightly, but magazines fell sharply. Digital publishing is now almost the same size as print books.

Compared with the year before

The total publishing market has fallen for four years in a row and is now back to about the same size as in 2019, before the pandemic.

Print is shrinking, but books are not

Almost all of the drop in print sales came from magazines. It seems that the place people go for information has shifted from magazines to smartphones. Print books, on the other hand, held at 593.9 billion yen, almost the same as the year before. For books meant to be read slowly and carefully, people still choose paper.

A note on the numbers

  • These are estimated sales figures from the Research Institute for Publications, not a full count of actual sales
  • Digital publishing is the total of digital manga, e-books and digital magazines

FAQ

How much have print book sales fallen?

In 2025 they were 964.7 billion yen, less than 40% of the 1996 peak (about 2.6 trillion yen). It is the first time they have fallen below 1 trillion yen since 1975.

Are children reading less?

In 2024, 52.7% of primary, middle and high school students read for 0 minutes a day. See more than half of school students read for 0 minutes a day.

Summary

  • In 2025, sales of print publications in Japan fell below 1 trillion yen for the first time in 50 years
  • The decline was mainly in magazines; print books were roughly flat
  • Digital publishing grew to 581.5 billion yen
