How many notifications does your phone receive each day? What the research measured

November 9, 2026 · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

How many notifications reach your phone in a day? Studies that actually logged people's phones found an average of roughly 64 to 90 a day for adults, and a median of 237 for teenagers in the US. None of these studies is large, and they cover different countries and ages, but they agree on one thing: our phones call for our attention dozens to hundreds of times a day. Notifications per day, study by study

All three studies counted notifications from phone logs, not from questionnaires. But the people, the years and the way the numbers are summarized (average or median) all differ, so the figures cannot be compared directly.

The 2014 study: 63.5 a day, mostly messages and email

A research team at a Spanish telecom company (Pielot et al., 2014) installed a logging app on the Android phones of 15 adults (aged 24 to 43, living in North America or Europe) and recorded every notification they received for one week. The paper's abstract gives an average of 63.5 a day (the body of the paper counts 6,854 notifications over 105 person-days, or 65.3 per person per day).

The median time before a notification was viewed was a few minutes for messages (6.6 minutes on weekdays, 3.5 minutes at weekends). In 69.2% of cases the screen was off when the notification arrived, which suggests notifications often prompted people to pick up the phone. Days with more notifications also tended to come with more negative feelings.

The 2019 study: about 90 a day among 238 iPhone users

A team of UK university researchers (Ellis et al., 2019) collected iPhone "Screen Time" records from 238 people (average age 31.9). Averaged over six days, they received 90.13 notifications a day, picked up their phones 85.84 times, and used them for 232.66 minutes. About 77% of the participants were recruited through an online research platform.

It is interesting that pickups (about 86) and notifications (about 90) are so close, but the study did not check whether each pickup followed a notification.

The 2023 report: a median of 237 for US teens

A report by the US nonprofit Common Sense Media (2023) installed a logging app on the phones (Android only) of 203 people aged 11 to 17 and tracked a week of use (participants were recruited from August to November 2022).

  • The median number of notifications received per day was 237. Participants saw or engaged with about a quarter of them (median 46)
  • At the top end, some phones received more than 4,500 notifications in a day
  • About 23% of notifications arrived during school hours, and about 5% at night
  • Snapchat and Discord sent the most notifications

Numbers varied widely: 20% of participants received 100 or fewer a day, while another 20% received more than 500. Note that this is not a nationally representative survey, and iPhone users were not included.

What happened when notifications were switched off for 24 hours?

In an experiment by one of the same researchers (Pielot and Rello, 2017), 30 people (aged 19 to 56, mainly office workers and students) switched off notifications on all their devices for 24 hours, and the researchers compared that day with an ordinary one.

  • The upside: people felt less distracted and more productive
  • The downside: some felt anxious about not being able to reply quickly, or less connected to their group
  • 73.3% said they intended to switch off some notifications, and two years later half were still doing so

Still, this was a small group of 30, and feelings were measured only by what participants reported. Choosing which notifications to keep may be more realistic than switching them all off. We explain how to sort them in which phone notifications to turn off.

FAQ

How many notifications does the average person get a day?

It depends on the study. A 2014 study that logged 15 adults found an average of 63.5 a day, and a 2019 study of 238 iPhone users found an average of about 90. A 2023 report on 203 US 11- to 17-year-olds found a median of 237.

Why do I sometimes feel a notification that never came?

This is called "phantom vibration," and many people experience it. We summarize the research in felt your phone buzz when it didn't?

Summary

  • In phone logs, adults received an average of roughly 64 to 90 notifications a day, and US teens a median of 237
  • Most are messages and email, and they are often viewed within minutes
  • All of these studies are small and cover different people, so treat the numbers as a rough guide
