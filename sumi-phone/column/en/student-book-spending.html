University students' spending on books has fallen below 1,000 yen a month, and 92% have used generative AI

November 5, 2026 · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

The amount university students in Japan spend on books each month has fallen below 1,000 yen. In the 2025 survey by the National Federation of University Co-operative Associations (the national body of campus co-op stores), students living with their families spent 970 yen and students living on their own 990 yen. The year before, the figures were 1,450 yen and 1,500 yen, so spending fell by more than 30% in a single year. The same survey found that the share of students who have used generative AI rose to 92.2%.

The short version

  • University students' book spending fell below 1,000 yen a month for the first time since 2016
  • In one year, it fell by 480 yen for students living with family and 510 yen for students living on their own
  • The share who have used generative AI rose from 68.2% to 92.2% in one year

It seems likely that more students are turning to generative AI and the internet instead of buying books for research and reports. But this survey alone cannot tell us why book spending fell.

About the survey

  • This is the National Federation of University Co-operative Associations' "61st Survey of Student Life"
  • It was carried out online in October and November 2025, with 13,277 respondents from 30 university co-ops
