How to set your phone screen to black and white (grayscale), and what the research says

October 24, 2026 (updated October 25, 2026) · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

When you turn your phone screen black and white, the color drains out of app icons, social media photos and videos. Is that enough to make you open your phone less on autopilot? In a Dutch study, just one week of black and white cut daily screen time by about 20 minutes on average. The setting takes a minute, and you can switch back at any time. This article covers the steps for iPhone and Android, and what the research found.

The short version

  • Black and white cut daily screen time by about 20 minutes (a one-week study of 84 people)
  • But people did not open their phones any less often. Black and white alone does not get rid of the habit of reaching for your phone
  • iPhone: Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Color Filters → Grayscale
  • Android: Accessibility → Color and motion → Color correction → Grayscale
  • If black and white all the time is too much, use it only at night or only while you work

What changes when you go black and white (the research)

Researchers in the Netherlands (Dekker and Baumgartner) recorded how 84 people used their phones for two weeks. In the first week, they used their phones as usual; in the second week, they set their screens to black and white.

  • Daily screen time fell by about 20 minutes on average
  • The number of times people unlocked their phones did not change
  • People felt more in control of how they used their phones, and felt less that they were overusing them and less stressed
  • Productivity and sleep quality did not change

In other words, black and white cuts down how long you linger once you open your phone, but the habit of opening it stays. The researchers themselves described the unchanged number of unlocks as a "deeply rooted checking habit." Black and white works best combined with other measures.

How to turn an iPhone black and white

Go to Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Color Filters, turn it on, and choose Grayscale.

To switch back, turn Color Filters off on the same screen. Black and white only changes how the screen looks; your photos and videos are still saved in color.

To switch with one tap, go to Settings → Accessibility → Accessibility Shortcut and choose Color Filters. Then quickly pressing the side button three times (the Home button on iPhone SE), a "triple-click," switches between black and white and color.

How to turn an Android phone black and white

Go to Settings → Accessibility → Color and motion → Color correction, choose Grayscale, and turn on "Use color correction."

To switch quickly, swipe down twice from the top of the screen to open Quick Settings, tap the pencil icon, and add the Color correction tile. Menu names can differ slightly depending on the phone model.

How to go black and white only at night

For some people, black and white all day makes maps and photos hard to see, so they can't keep it up. If that's you, use black and white only at the times you tend to pick up your phone.

  • Android: in Settings → Digital Wellbeing & parental controls → Bedtime mode, set a schedule and turn on grayscale in the screen options. Your phone will turn black and white automatically at the set time
  • iPhone: use the shortcut above (triple-click) and switch it yourself before bed or when you get into bed

If you can't put your phone down at night, see also how to stop using your phone before bed.

Who black and white works less well for, and what to combine it with

As the study showed, black and white does not reduce how often you reach for your phone. If what sets you off is notifications or boredom, black and white alone is not enough.

  • If notifications make you open your phone: cut back your notifications. Doing this together with black and white reduces both your reasons to open the phone and your reasons to linger
  • If videos and short clips eat your time: combine it with app time limits (setting up Screen Time, how to step away from short videos)
  • If you use photos or maps a lot: use the shortcut to switch back to color only when you need it

Summary

  • One study found that black and white cut daily screen time by about 20 minutes
  • It does not reduce how often you open your phone, so combine it with cutting notifications, setting time limits and so on
  • What to do today: set grayscale through Color Filters on iPhone or Color correction on Android, and try it for a week first

A black-and-white screen works by making your phone "boring," so it is easier to turn your eyes to the world outside the screen. Soto Phone is a phone in development with a calm black-and-white screen from the start, and no social media or games.
