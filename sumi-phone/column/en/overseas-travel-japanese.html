14.73 million Japanese people went abroad in 2025, back to about 70% of pre-COVID levels

November 14, 2026 · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

In 2025, Japanese nationals made 14,731,615 departures from Japan. That was up 13.3% from the year before, but still only about 70% of the record 20.08 million in 2019. Because of COVID-19, the number had dropped to 510,000 in 2021. This summary is based on statistics from the Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) and the Immigration Services Agency of Japan.

Japanese departures over time

The number of Japanese people going abroad rose sharply in the 1990s, from 11 million in 1990 to 17.82 million in 2000. After that, with some down years along the way, it stayed between 15 and 19 million in most years, and in 2019 it passed 20 million for the first time.

In 2003 departures were 13.3 million, down 19.5% from the year before. They also fell in 2008 and 2009. Overseas travel can drop sharply from one year to the next.

In 2021, departures were only about 2.6% of the 2019 level. From March 2022, entry restrictions were eased step by step and flights increased, and departures came back too (Immigration Services Agency). But the pace of recovery has slowed, and even in 2025 departures were only about 70% of pre-COVID levels.

Meanwhile, foreign visitors to Japan reached a record 42.68 million in 2025. Japanese departures were about a third of that. For how the cost of domestic travel has changed, see the average cost of a domestic trip.

Young people traveling abroad

The Immigration Services Agency's "Immigration Control Statistics: Summary of Results" includes departures by age. In 2024, the biggest increase from the previous year was among people in their 20s, up 40.0% (799,553 more). Next came teenagers, up 38.8%, and people aged 60 and over, up 37.8%.

Among women, people in their 20s make up more than one in four departures. Still, a chart in the same document shows departures by people in their 20s at about 2.8 million in 2024, compared with about 3.8 million in 2019. Young people's overseas travel has not returned to pre-COVID levels either.

Things to keep in mind

  • Japanese departures are a total count based on the Ministry of Justice's immigration control statistics. A person who leaves Japan three times in a year is counted three times
  • The figures include people leaving for work or study, not only for travel
  • The age figures are for 2024. We could not find 2025 figures by age this time

FAQ

How many Japanese people traveled abroad in 2025?

There were 14,731,615 Japanese departures (total count), up 13.3% from the year before.

Has overseas travel returned to pre-COVID levels?

Not yet. Compared with the record 20.08 million in 2019, 2025 was about 73%.

Summary

  • Japanese departures in 2025 were 14.73 million, about 70% of pre-COVID levels
  • From 510,000 in 2021, departures have risen four years in a row
  • The biggest increase in 2024 was among people in their 20s, and 27.8% of women who went abroad were in their 20s
