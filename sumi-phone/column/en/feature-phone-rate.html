How many people still use a feature phone? Usage by age and over time

November 1, 2026 · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

In fiscal 2025 (Japan's fiscal year runs April–March), 10.2% of people of all ages used a feature phone, often called a "garakei" in Japan. That is one in ten. In fiscal 2020 the figure was 18.1%, so it has almost halved in five years. Smartphone use was 95.9%, so nearly everyone has one. This summary is based on a survey by the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC).

The short version

  • Feature phone use stands at 10.2% (18.1% in fiscal 2020 → 10.2% in fiscal 2025)
  • It rises with age: 20.9% for people in their 70s, 3.6% for teens
  • Some people use both a smartphone and a feature phone, so the totals add up to more than 100%

Usage over time

Over the same five years, smartphone use rose from 88.0% to 95.9%.

Usage by age

Even among people in their 20s to 40s, around 5–8% use a feature phone. This includes people who carry one alongside a smartphone.

If you are choosing a feature phone now

  • Choose a 4G model: Japanese carriers have shut down their 3G networks. If you buy now, get a model that supports 4G (VoLTE) (feature phones you can use in 2026)
  • Decide whether you need LINE and maps: there are also "flip-style smartphones" with physical buttons that can run LINE (Japan's most-used messaging app) (what is a minimal phone?)
  • Consider carrying two phones: leave the smartphone at home and take the feature phone when you go out (how to use two phones)

Summary

  • 10.2% of people use a feature phone, almost half the level of five years ago
  • 20.9% of people in their 70s, 3.6% of teens
  • Smartphone use stands at 95.9%
