What kids do online: how elementary, junior high and high school students use the internet

October 29, 2026 · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

More than 90% of elementary, junior high and high school students who use the internet watch videos online. Next come searching and looking things up, games, and messaging on social media and the like. This summary of how use differs by school level is based on the Children and Families Agency's survey for fiscal 2025 (Japan's fiscal year runs April–March).

The short version

  • Overall: watching videos 92.1%, searching and looking things up 86.0%, games 84.9%, communication 77.4%
  • Elementary school students mostly watch videos and play games. Messaging on social media and the like is 49.5%
  • By high school, use broadens: messaging 92.7%, music 90.1%, maps 65.1% and more

Overall: the most common uses

These are the answers of 3,030 young people aged 10 to 17 who use the internet on any of seven types of device, including smartphones, game consoles and TVs. Multiple answers were allowed.

"Communication" means social media such as LINE (Japan's most-used messaging app), Instagram and X, plus email. "Live streaming video," "e-books" and "generative AI" are new items added from fiscal 2025.

Games are the only use that dips slightly in high school. For almost everything else, the share rises with each school level.

From elementary to junior high, "messaging" grows the most

Messaging on social media and the like rises 35 points, from 49.5% of elementary school students to 84.8% of junior high students. This overlaps with more children getting their own smartphone when they start junior high (74.9% of elementary school students, 95.4% of junior high students).

Looking at smartphones only

Among children who use the internet on a smartphone (2,401 people), the top uses were messaging 68.2%, games 66.5% and search 62.7% for elementary school students; messaging 90.0%, videos 86.6% and search 86.0% for junior high students; and messaging 93.2%, videos 91.2% and music 89.2% for high school students. On smartphones, messaging on social media and the like is the top use at every school level.

Summary

  • Students' top online uses are videos 92.1%, search 86.0% and games 84.9%
  • The higher the school level, the more use spreads to messaging, music, maps, shopping and more
  • On smartphones, messaging on social media and the like is the top use at every school level
