How many people exercise at least once a week? Rates by age and gender

October 30, 2026 · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

51.7% of people aged 20 and over do exercise or sports at least one day a week. That's roughly one in two. By age, people in their 30s had the lowest rate, and among women in their 30s it was 40.3%. This summary is based on the Japan Sports Agency's survey for fiscal 2025 (Japan's fiscal year runs April to March).

The short version

  • 51.7% of people aged 20 and over exercise at least one day a week (down 0.8 points from the year before)
  • Men 55.0%, women 48.8%. The gap between men and women is especially large from the teens through the 40s
  • 26.9% have kept up exercise that works up a light sweat, for 30 minutes or more, at least two days a week, for a year or longer

Rates by age

Rates are low from the 30s through the 50s and rise from the 60s on. In this survey, the top reasons people gave for exercising less were "I'm busy with work," "it's too much bother," and "my physical strength has declined." People in their 70s were at 65.1%, higher than any other age group.

The trend

The share of people aged 20 and over exercising at least one day a week peaked at 59.9% in fiscal 2020 and has fallen since (52.3% in fiscal 2022, 52.0% in fiscal 2023, 52.5% in fiscal 2024 and 51.7% in fiscal 2025).

What kind of exercise people do

The most common activity was walking (including strolls, wandering about and getting off a stop early to walk), done by 23,962 of the 40,000 respondents. Next came using the stairs. The top activities are ones that need no special equipment or place.

Among walkers, those who mostly walk with other people had a higher average happiness score than those who walk alone (7.2 versus 6.7).

Making time for exercise

  • Start with walking: It's the activity the most people do. Walking one extra stop or skipping the elevator counts as real exercise (the benefits of walking)
  • Swap some of your phone time: Take just 30 minutes of your daily phone time and spend it outside, and you'll reach the guideline of 30 minutes, two days a week
  • Do it with someone: Having someone to walk with makes it easier to keep going and more satisfying

How the survey was done

It was an online survey of people aged 18 to 79 across Japan, with 40,000 respondents (November 2025). The survey switched from face-to-face interviews to online in fiscal 2016, so its figures can't be directly compared with those from before then.

Summary

  • 51.7% of adults exercise at least one day a week
  • People in their 30s have the lowest rate, and women in their 30s are at 40.3%
  • Walking is the most common activity
