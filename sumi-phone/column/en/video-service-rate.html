Video service usage rates in Japan ranked: YouTube, Netflix, TVer and more

November 1, 2026 · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

The most widely used video service in Japan is YouTube, with a usage rate of 84.4%. It is followed by Amazon Prime Video (40.6%), TVer (35.9%; free online streaming of programs from Japan's TV networks) and Netflix (30.8%). Among people in their teens through 40s, YouTube's usage rate is above 90%. This summary is based on a survey by the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC).

The short version

  • YouTube 84.4%, Amazon Prime Video 40.6%, TVer 35.9%, Netflix 30.8%
  • Streaming services such as Netflix are used by 56.6% across all ages, up from 39.8% five years earlier
  • TV stations' online program services such as TVer more than doubled in five years, from 19.8% to 43.1%

Usage rates by service

YouTube is first in every age group. In second place was Amazon Prime Video for people in their teens to 40s, TVer for people in their 50s and 60s, and NHK Plus (the public broadcaster's streaming service) for people in their 70s.

Avoiding watching too much

  • Turn off autoplay: just stopping the next video from starting on its own gives you a natural place to stop (settings to stop watching too much YouTube)
  • Decide how many videos first: open the app only after deciding "just this one today"
  • Watch out for short videos: the shorter each video, the harder it is to notice the total time (how to quit short videos)

Summary

  • The top video service is YouTube (84.4%)
  • Paid streaming such as Netflix rose about 17 points in five years, to 56.6%
  • TV stations' streaming services such as TVer more than doubled in five years
