The average phone bill in Japan: monthly cost by type of carrier (service plus handset)

October 29, 2026 · By Kazuma Sasaki (Founder, Soto Phone)

On average, people in Japan pay 8,802 yen a month for their phone with a major carrier, and 4,330 yen with a budget SIM provider (MVNO). Both figures combine service and call charges with installment payments for the handset. This summary of the averages and breakdowns by type of carrier is based on a survey MMD Research Institute carried out in August 2026 (published in September 2026).

The short version

  • Average monthly cost (service and calls plus handset): 8,802 yen with the four major carriers, 6,651 yen on online-only plans, 5,411 yen with sub-brands and 4,330 yen with MVNOs
  • With the four major carriers, service is 4,427 yen and the handset 4,375 yen, roughly half each
  • Monthly costs rose for every type of carrier compared with the year before (September 2025)

Monthly cost by type of carrier

MMD Research Institute ran a screening survey of 40,000 people aged 18 to 79 and tallied the answers of the 36,830 who use a smartphone, weighted to match the actual share of users.

Which companies and plans fall into which category depends on the survey's own definitions.

Service charges vs. handset payments

TotalService and callsHandset
Four major carriers8,802 yen4,427 yen4,375 yen
Online-only plans6,651 yen3,141 yen3,510 yen
Sub-brands5,411 yen2,718 yen2,693 yen
MVNOs4,330 yen1,681 yen2,649 yen

For every type of carrier, the handset makes up about half of the monthly cost. With MVNOs, the handset actually costs more than the service.

Change from the previous year

TotalService and callsHandset
Four major carriers+251 yen+7 yen+244 yen
Online-only plans+77 yen+61 yen+16 yen
Sub-brands+241 yen+121 yen+119 yen
MVNOs+49 yen+69 yen−20 yen

These are the differences from the September 2025 survey. For the four major carriers, almost all of the increase came from the handset.

How much data people use

The most common plan size differed by type of carrier.

  • Four major carriers: large data plans (38.3%)
  • Online-only plans: medium data plans (63.7%)
  • Sub-brands: small data plans (55.8%)
  • MVNOs: small data plans (66.7%)

Also, 82.1% of people knew how much they pay their carrier each month, and 47.5% were paying for their handset in installments (excluding "don't know" answers).

A note on the numbers

  • These are amounts the respondents reported, and they may differ from what is on the bill
  • How family discounts and points rewards were counted varies from person to person

Summary

  • The average monthly phone cost is 8,802 yen with the four major carriers and 4,330 yen with MVNOs (August 2026)
  • For every type of carrier, the handset makes up about half of the monthly cost
  • Monthly costs rose for every type of carrier compared with the year before
