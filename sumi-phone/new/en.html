Questions

How much will it cost?

We’re planning a price between ¥50,000 and ¥70,000. People on the list will hear the final price first, together with the phone plan we’re preparing.

When will it ship?

We’ll set the launch date after the prototype trial.

Will LINE work?

We’ll decide how to handle LINE before launch and let you know. We’re testing a way to include it with limited notifications.

Do I need to keep my smartphone too?

Either way works. You can live with Soto alone, or leave your smartphone at home.

What about my carrier and SIM?

We’re making it work with the SIM from your current carrier in Japan.
