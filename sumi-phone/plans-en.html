Plans

The Soto plan is a phone plan where your next bill goes down the more partner shops you visit. You can also use the SIM from your current carrier.

How your bill is worked out

Visit a partner shop and you get a stamp. Next month's bill is the monthly fee minus your stamps.

Collecting stamps

  1. Pick a place: study cafés, bookshops, bathhouses and other partner shops appear on the Soto Phone map
  2. Tap your Soto Phone at the shop: you get one stamp, up to one a day
  3. It comes off next month's bill: stamps from every shop go on the same card

Why it can be cheaper

Partner shopPays Soto a fee only when a customer walks in
→
SotoPuts that fee toward your discount
→
YouYour next bill goes down

A shop that pays for flyers or online ads never knows whether anyone will come. With Soto, it pays only when a customer actually walks in, so it replaces advertising. The discount comes from that fee. Soto isn't selling at a loss.
