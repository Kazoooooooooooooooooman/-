On a weekday, people in Japan spend about 2 hours a day online on mobile devices such as smartphones, averaged across all ages. Teens spend the longest, at about 3 hours 22 minutes, followed by people in their 20s at about 3 hours 14 minutes. We have pulled together the figures by age group from the report of a survey that Japan's Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC) carried out in December 2025 (published in June 2026).

The short version

  • Internet use on mobile devices (smartphones and mobile phones) averages 120.5 minutes on a weekday across all ages
  • Teens average 201.5 minutes and people in their 20s 194.4 minutes. People in their 40s (150.7 minutes) spend longer than those in their 30s (143.0 minutes)
  • Compared with the previous year, time went up for people in their 30s, 40s and 70s, and down for those in their 50s and 60s

By age: teens and people in their 20s top 3 hours

For the MIC Institute for Information and Communications Policy's "Survey on Time Spent Using Information and Communications Media and Information Behavior," 1,800 people aged 13 to 79 recorded what they did each day in a diary, in 15-minute blocks. Average weekday time spent online on smartphones or mobile phones was as follows.

Teens and people in their 20s spend more than 3 hours a day even on weekdays. People in their 40s spending longer than those in their 30s is the reverse of the previous year (30s: 132.7 minutes, 40s: 126.6 minutes).

The biggest increase was among people in their 40s, up 24.1 minutes in one year. More than 90% of teens, people in their 20s and people in their 40s go online with a smartphone on weekdays.

Compared with computers

In the same survey, weekday time spent online on a computer was 50.3 minutes across all ages. In every age group, people spent longer on mobile devices such as smartphones than on computers. Teens spent only 13.1 minutes on computers on weekdays, which shows that they do almost all of their internet use on smartphones.

Things to keep in mind about the numbers

  • These averages include people who did not use them at all, counted as 0 minutes
  • "Mobile devices" combines smartphones and basic mobile phones (feature phones). Phone calls and camera use without the internet are not included
  • Other surveys (by Hakuhodo, the Children and Families Agency and others) count time differently, so their figures cannot be compared side by side

Figures from other surveys are collected in average daily smartphone use in Japan. For weekends, see smartphone time on weekends and holidays.

Summary

  • Internet use on mobile devices averages 120.5 minutes on a weekday across all ages (fiscal 2025)
  • Teens average 201.5 minutes and people in their 20s 194.4 minutes, both over 3 hours
  • People in their 40s rose 24.1 minutes in one year, overtaking those in their 30s
