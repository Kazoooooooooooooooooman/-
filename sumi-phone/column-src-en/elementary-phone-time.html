Elementary school children aged 10 and over spend an average of about 3 hours 54 minutes a day online on weekdays. We pulled together time by gender, the share using smartphones, and what they use them for, from the report of a survey the Children and Families Agency carried out in November and December 2025 (published March 2026).

The short version

  • Elementary school children (aged 10 and over) spend an average of 233.6 minutes (about 3 hours 54 minutes) online on a weekday
  • Boys spend 248.4 minutes and girls 217.3 minutes, so boys spend about 31 minutes longer
  • 61.7% are online for 3 hours or more. 49.0% of elementary school children who use the internet go online with a smartphone

About 3 hours 54 minutes online on a weekday

The Children and Families Agency's "Survey on the Internet Use Environment of Young People" asks 10- to 17-year-olds how long they use the internet on a weekday, device by device. Among elementary school children who use the internet (847 children), the average was 233.6 minutes.

This time is the total across seven kinds of devices, including smartphones, tablets issued by school, game consoles and TVs, and it includes use for study. 61.7% of elementary school children were online for "3 hours or more".

By purpose, "hobbies and entertainment" took 161.5 minutes for boys and 128.2 minutes for girls, so boys spent longer. "Study, learning and educational use" took 41.9 minutes for boys and 52.1 minutes for girls, so girls spent longer (each is the average among children who use the internet for that purpose).

About half go online with a smartphone

Of elementary school children who use the internet, 49.0% go online with a smartphone (one with its own contract). The average time online on a smartphone is 109.1 minutes. Other common devices are computers or tablets issued by school (72.7%), game consoles (71.7%) and TVs (69.4%).

What they use smartphones for, from most to least common:

  1. Watching videos (74.2%)
  2. Messaging on social media, email and so on (68.2%)
  3. Games (66.5%)
  4. Searching and looking things up (62.7%)
  5. Listening to music (46.5%)

8.2% of elementary school children use generative AI (such as ChatGPT) on a smartphone.

More than 80% of families have rules

83.8% of elementary school children said their family has rules about internet use, higher than junior high school students (74.5%) and high school students (44.3%). How to set rules is covered in how to make family phone rules without the fights.

Things to keep in mind with these numbers

  • The survey covers elementary school children aged 10 and over, so the lower grades are not included
  • Use of devices without the internet (such as offline games) is not included
  • The times are reported by the children themselves, so they may differ from actual time
