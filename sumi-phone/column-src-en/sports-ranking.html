Among people aged 10 and over, the most common sport done in the past year was "walking and light exercise" (44.3%), far ahead of second-placed "training with equipment" (12.9%). Only walking and cycling increased from five years earlier, and almost every other sport declined. This summary is based on the Survey on Time Use and Leisure Activities by the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC).

The short version

  • 1st: walking and light exercise 44.3%. 2nd: training with equipment 12.9%. 3rd: jogging and marathons 11.1%
  • Only walking (+3.0 points) and cycling (+0.3 points) increased from five years earlier
  • Bowling fell from 12.7% to 5.1% and swimming from 11.0% to 5.7%, both less than half

Sports ranking (2021)

The participation rate is the share of people aged 10 and over who did the sport in the past year (October 2020 to October 2021). From 11th place down: yoga 5.5%, bowling 5.1%, table tennis 4.9%, soccer 4.7%, basketball 3.6% and volleyball 3.5%.

The only two that grew are ones you can do on your own close to home. Sports where people gather at a facility, such as a pool or bowling alley, fell sharply. The fact that the survey period included two COVID-19 states of emergency is likely to have played a part.
