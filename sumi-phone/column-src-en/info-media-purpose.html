The internet when you want to know fast, TV when you want information you can trust. In a fiscal 2025 survey by Japan's Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC), the medium people use split clearly by purpose. The internet is used most "to find out quickly what is happening in the world" (55.4%), but TV is chosen most often "to get reliable information" (50.3%).

The short version

  • Breaking news: the internet (55.4%). Reliable information: TV (50.3%)
  • Hobby and entertainment information: the internet (70.8%). Work and research: also the internet (81.2%)
  • For "finding out quickly", people in their teens to 50s choose the internet, and people in their 60s and 70s choose TV

Respondents chose only one medium; these are results for all ages (13 to 79). For work and research, books (4.2%) were chosen more often than for other purposes.

Getting reliable information

By age group, people in their teens and in their 50s to 70s use TV most, while people in their 20s to 40s use the internet most. Newspapers, at 13.0%, score higher here than for other purposes.

By importance

  • TV has the highest share of people calling it "important as a source of information", at 80.6%
  • The internet has the highest share calling it "important as entertainment", at 80.7%
  • On both measures, the internet ranks highest among people in their teens to 40s, and TV among people in their 50s to 70s

How to live with information

  • Check breaking news later: confirm what you learn online against TV, newspapers or official announcements
  • Cut down the feed: looking things up yourself when you want to know takes less of your time

Summary

  • The internet for breaking news, TV for reliable information
