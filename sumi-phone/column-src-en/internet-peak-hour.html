The time of day when the most people are online is the 9 p.m. hour. On weekdays between 9 and 10 p.m., 40.1% of people aged 13 to 79 were using the internet. Among teens and people in their 20s, it is more than half. TV peaks in the 8 p.m. hour, and from 9 p.m. the internet overtakes TV. This summary is based on a survey by Japan's Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC).

The short version

  • Internet use peaks in the 9 p.m. hour on both weekdays and weekends
  • The second peak is the weekday 12 p.m. hour (34.2%). Many people go online during their lunch break
  • TV peaks in the 8 p.m. hour. From 9 p.m. on, more people are online than watching TV

Internet use by time of day (weekdays, all ages)

From the 7 a.m. hour through the 11 p.m. hour, more than 20% of people are online at any time. There are two peaks: the 12 p.m. hour at lunchtime, and the 8 to 10 p.m. hours in the evening.

For teens, the highest hour is 10 p.m., at 55.4%. For people in their 20s, 40% are still online in the 11 p.m. hour, and more than 20% in the midnight hour.

What about weekends?

On weekends the peak is also the 9 p.m. hour, at 39.7%. On weekends, the 9 to 11 a.m. hours and the afternoon are higher than on weekdays, and there is no lunchtime peak in the 12 p.m. hour as there is on weekdays. In the 9 p.m. hour on weekends, 53.2% of people in their 20s and 54.4% of people in their 40s are online.

Reading these numbers

  • These are the shares of people who used the internet at all during that hour (the "participation rate"). They do not mean people were online for the whole hour
  • They include use on computers and tablets, not just phones. They also include use for work and study
  • The survey was carried out in fiscal 2025 and covers people aged 13 to 79

FAQ

When is the internet used most?

In the 9 p.m. hour, on both weekdays and weekends. 40.1% of people were online in that hour on weekdays, and 39.7% on weekends.

How many people use their phones before bed?

On weekdays, 26.2% of people are online in the 11 p.m. hour and 10.6% in the midnight hour. Among people in their 20s, 42.4% are online in the 11 p.m. hour. We cover phone use before bed in the effects of using your phone before bed.

How can I cut down on phone use at night?

The most reliable way is to put your phone out of reach during the 9 p.m. peak: leave it in another room, or set a fixed place to charge it.

Summary

  • Internet use peaks in the 9 p.m. hour (40.1% on weekdays)
  • Among teens and people in their 20s and 40s, more than half are online in the 9 p.m. hour
  • After 9 p.m., more people are online than watching TV
