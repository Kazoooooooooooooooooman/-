The short answer: yes, you can still use a flip phone (garakei, as Japan's classic feature phones are called) in 2026. But only a "4G keitai" (sometimes called a garaho), which runs on the 4G network, will work. Old flip phones that ran on 3G stopped working with every carrier in Japan when docomo shut down its 3G network on March 31, 2026. This article covers the models you can choose from today, typical prices, and what to watch for with LINE and budget SIMs.

The short version

  • 3G flip phones stopped working in Japan from April 2026. Only 4G feature phones (garaho) work now
  • The main models include docomo's "Raku-Raku Phone F-41F" and "DIGNO Keitai KY-42C", au's "Kantan Keitai Light KYF43", and SoftBank's "AQUOS Keitai 4" and "DIGNO Keitai 4"

3G flip phones no longer work

Most old flip phones ran on a network technology called 3G. Carriers shut down 3G one after another, and with docomo (FOMA and i-mode) on March 31, 2026, the last 3G network in Japan was gone.

CarrierDate 3G ended
au (CDMA 1X WIN)March 31, 2022
SoftBankApril 15, 2024 (July 31 in Ishikawa Prefecture)
docomo (FOMA and i-mode)March 31, 2026

Even among 4G models, older phones that cannot use a system called "VoLTE" for calls lost the ability to make calls when 3G ended. At docomo, a fault also appeared from the day after the 3G shutdown in which calls would not connect on some 4G feature phones, and users were told to change a setting on the phone (calls over the 4G network). If you use an older 4G feature phone, now is the time to check that setting and also think about replacing the phone.

Typical prices

4G feature phones have cheap plans that cover calls and a little data.

  • docomo "Keitai Plan": 1,507 yen a month (including tax). Data up to 100 MB (after that, up to 128 kbps). Calls to family are free; other calls cost 22 yen per 30 seconds. A price rise of 110 yen a month (including tax) from December 1, 2026 has been announced
  • au "Keitai Plan" and SoftBank's basic plan: similarly, from the 1,000-yen range a month. Carriers kept revising prices through 2026, so check the latest amounts on the official pages before you sign up

If you make a lot of calls, adding a flat-rate calling option (for example, docomo's free calls up to 5 minutes for 880 yen a month) often still comes out cheaper than a smartphone.

Can you use LINE?

LINE (Japan's most-used messaging app) works on some 4G feature phones. But keep two things in mind.

  1. Whether it works depends on the model: in the past, LINE has stopped working on older models first. A 4G feature phone does not automatically mean LINE will work
  2. LINE's supported environments change in early November 2026: LINE's official help pages say support for older versions of the app will end in early November 2026. Models running older versions of Android may no longer be able to use it

If you need LINE, always ask the carrier's shop staff before you buy: "Will LINE still work on this model after November?" If you really need LINE and the model does not support it, you can split things up, for example by receiving LINE messages on a smartphone or computer at home.

What to watch for with a budget SIM

If you want to lower your monthly bill even more, you can use a 4G feature phone with a budget SIM. But there are more things to check than with a smartphone.

  • SIM lock: models released before October 2021 may need to be SIM-unlocked
  • Compatibility: check whether the model is on the budget SIM provider's published list of tested devices. Feature phones are often not listed
  • VoLTE: without VoLTE support, you cannot make calls
  • App-based procedures: some providers, such as povo, require a smartphone app to sign up or set up

If it is your first time, signing up with a carrier together with a feature phone plan is the most reliable way.

How many people choose a flip phone?

In a January 2026 survey by the Mobile Society Research Institute, 98.3% of people who own a mobile phone used a smartphone. That works out to just under 2% using a mobile phone other than a smartphone. It is a small number, but some people deliberately choose a flip phone because "calls and email are enough" or because they "want to spend less time looking at a smartphone".
