Time spent reading and posting on social media averages 36.2 minutes a day on weekdays across all ages. People in their 20s spend the longest, at 79.1 minutes on weekdays and 114.6 minutes on days off. We summarize the figures by age and gender from the report of a survey the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications carried out in December 2025 (published June 2026).

The short version

  • Social media time across all ages: 36.2 minutes on weekdays, 45.4 minutes on days off
  • People in their 20s spend the longest: 79.1 minutes on weekdays and 114.6 minutes (about 1 hour 55 minutes) on days off
  • Women spend longer than men; women in their 20s spend 126.9 minutes on days off

By age: people in their 20s and teens spend the longest

In the survey by the ministry's Institute for Information and Communications Policy, 1,800 people aged 13 to 79 recorded what they did each day in 15-minute blocks, and the time spent on each type of internet use was calculated. The average time spent "reading and posting on social media" was as follows.

In contrast, men spent longer on video sharing services (such as YouTube) and online games.

Video takes even longer than social media

In the same survey, the internet use that took the most time on weekdays across all ages was "watching video sharing services" (54.5 minutes), longer than social media (36.2 minutes). Things to keep in mind about these numbers

  • The averages include people who do not use social media, counted as 0 minutes
  • They include use on devices other than phones, such as computers
  • Where time spent on messaging apps such as LINE (Japan's most-used messaging app) is counted depends on how respondents recorded it
