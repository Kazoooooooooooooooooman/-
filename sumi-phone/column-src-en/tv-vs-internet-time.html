On weekdays, people of all ages in Japan spend an average of 156.1 minutes a day watching live TV and 183.9 minutes using the internet. But TV time is 43.4 minutes for teens and 313.5 minutes for people in their 70s, a gap of more than seven times. We compared the figures by age group using a fiscal 2025 survey by the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC). Japan's fiscal year runs April–March.

The short version

  • On weekdays, the all-age average is 156.1 minutes of TV and 183.9 minutes of internet. The internet comes out ahead
  • On weekdays, only people in their 60s and 70s spend more time on TV than on the internet. On weekends, TV leads from the 50s up
  • The biggest gap is among people in their 20s on weekends: 67.4 minutes of TV versus 324.5 minutes of internet

Weekdays: TV and internet by age

The survey asked 1,800 people aged 13 to 79 across Japan to record what they did in 15-minute blocks, like a diary, over two weekdays and one weekend day. The figures are averages that include people who did not use TV or the internet that day.

On weekends, the all-age averages for TV and the internet are almost level. People in their 20s spend 324.5 minutes online on weekends, more than five hours. We also cover weekend phone time in phone time on days off.

How to read "turning away from TV"

  • For people from their teens to their 40s, TV time is somewhere between less than half and about half of internet time
  • For people in their 60s and 70s, TV is still the medium they spend the most time with
  • Internet time includes time spent watching videos. Some people watch TV programs online, so we can't go as far as saying people have "stopped watching TV programs"

Whether people trust TV or the internet more is covered in how much people trust different media.

Summary

  • On weekdays, the all-age average is 156.1 minutes of TV and 183.9 minutes of internet
  • People from their teens to their 50s spend more time online; people in their 60s and up spend more time on TV (on weekends, TV leads from the 50s up)
  • Newspaper reading averages 8.8 minutes on weekdays, and 27.1 minutes even for people in their 70s
