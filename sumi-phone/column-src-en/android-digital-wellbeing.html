Android comes with a feature called Digital Wellbeing that helps you use your phone less. You can see how many minutes you spent in each app, set a daily limit per app, and turn the screen black and white at bedtime. Based on Google's official help pages, here are the steps for the main settings.

The short version

  • Start from the Settings app, then tap "Digital Wellbeing"
  • The first things to set up are "App timers" (a daily limit for each app) and "Bedtime mode"
  • Menu names and steps may differ depending on your phone model

First: open Digital Wellbeing

  1. Open the Settings app and tap "Digital Wellbeing"
  2. If you want an icon in your app list, turn on "Show icon in the app list"

The first time you use it, you need to set up your profile ("More" → "Manage your data").

Check how much time you spent

When you open Digital Wellbeing, a chart shows how you used your phone that day. Tap the chart to see:

  • Screen time: which apps were on screen, and for how long
  • Unlocks: how many times you unlocked your phone and opened each app
  • Notifications received: how many notifications arrived, and from which apps

Long-press your home screen, choose "Widgets" and add the screen time widget to see your top three apps at a glance every day (Android 9 and later).

App timers: set a daily limit for each app

  1. Go to Settings → "Digital Wellbeing" → "App timers"
  2. Tap "Set timer" next to the app you want to limit
  3. Choose how much time you allow per day, then tap "OK"

When you hit the limit, the app closes and its icon turns gray. Timers reset at midnight. They may not be available on work or school accounts.

In Chrome, you can also see time spent on each site and use "website timers" to set a limit per site (chart → Chrome icon).

Lock app limits with a PIN

  1. Go to Settings → "Digital Wellbeing" → "App limits" → "App limits PIN"
  2. Turn on the app limits PIN and unlock your phone
  3. Enter a 4-digit PIN, then enter it again to confirm

Without the PIN, app limits cannot be changed. If you tend to switch them off yourself, you can ask a family member to choose the PIN.

Bedtime mode: turn the screen black and white before bed

  1. Go to Settings → "Digital Wellbeing" → "Bedtime mode" → the bedtime routine
  2. Choose when it turns on: "Use a schedule" (times and days) or "Turn on while charging"

In the bedtime screen options, you can choose grayscale (black and white), dim the wallpaper, dark theme and more. You can also silence your phone during bedtime, or have the mode turn off automatically with your next alarm. We cover the effects of black and white in how to turn your phone black and white.

Focus mode: pause distracting apps

  1. Go to Settings → "Digital Wellbeing" → "Focus mode"
  2. Choose the apps you want to pause
  3. Tap "Turn on now". You can also use "Set a schedule" to turn it on automatically

While it is on, the apps you chose cannot be used and their notifications do not come through. If you need one briefly, choose a length of time under "Take a break".

Summary

  • Open Digital Wellbeing from the Settings app
  • Set a daily limit with app timers, and make them harder to switch off with a PIN
  • Use Bedtime mode and Focus mode to step away from your phone at set times
