At the end of December 2025, there were 47.35 million landline contracts in Japan, 2.8% fewer than a year earlier. The sharpest fall is in NTT East and West subscriber lines, which use traditional copper phone lines: down about 30%, from 15.73 million at the end of March 2021 to 10.78 million. This summary is based on quarterly data from the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC).

The short version

  • Landline contracts: 47.35 million (52.84 million at the end of March 2021)
  • Of these, 35.58 million are IP phones over fiber and similar lines (0ABJ-IP phones), and 10.78 million are NTT East and West subscriber lines
  • Mobile phone contracts: 229.27 million, about 4.8 times the number of landlines

0ABJ-IP phones are IP phone services with ordinary numbers that start with an area code, such as NTT's fiber-based "Hikari Denwa". Their numbers are roughly flat; the decline is mainly in subscriber lines. The contract figures include business and shop phones.

Landline market shares

  • NTT East and West (subscriber lines and IP phones combined): 61.5%
  • KDDI group: 22.3%

Do you need a home phone?

  • Useful if: someone in the family has no mobile phone, you want one number the whole family shares, or you use a fixed number for work
  • You can probably do without one if: everyone in the family has their own mobile and the landline only gets sales calls
  • If you keep it, protect it against scams: in Japan you can block incoming and outgoing international calls on a landline free of charge (phone scam losses and how to protect yourself)

Summary

  • There are 47.35 million landline contracts, and the number falls a little every year
  • NTT East and West subscriber lines have fallen about 30% in roughly five years, to 10.78 million
  • There are 229.27 million mobile phone contracts, about 4.8 times the number of landlines
