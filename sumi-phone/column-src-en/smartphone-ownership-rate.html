In Japan, 90.5% of households and 80.5% of people own a smartphone. Based on the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications' "Communications Usage Trend Survey," which looked at the situation as of the end of August 2024 (published May 2025), here is how widespread smartphones are by household, individual, age and prefecture.

The short version

  • 90.5% of households own a smartphone. That is almost the same as the year before, and about the same share as households with a TV
  • 80.5% of individuals own a smartphone, and the figure is rising. Ownership of mobile phones other than smartphones (so-called garakei, or basic feature phones) is 16.8% and falling
  • Among people aged 6 to 12 and 80 and over, more than 20% own neither a mobile phone nor a smartphone

Ownership by household and individual

A household counts if even one family member owns one, so the household figure is higher than the individual one. Looking at individuals, 4 in 5 people own a smartphone.

The feature phone figure is for "mobile phones (excluding smartphones)." Some people own both a smartphone and a feature phone, so the two figures don't add up to the total. We cover the current state of feature phones in can you still use a feature phone in 2026?

By age: children and people 80 and over are the least likely to own one

The ministry summarizes the trends by age as follows:

  • Among people aged 6 to 12 and 80 and over, more than 20% own neither a mobile phone nor a smartphone
  • 90% of people in each age group from 20 to 59, and about 80% of people aged 60 to 69, use the internet on a smartphone
  • 85.6% of people use the internet overall. In each age group from 13 to 69, it is more than 90%

As a device for going online, smartphones are 27.6 points ahead of computers.

Reading these numbers

  • The Communications Usage Trend Survey reflects the situation as of the end of August 2024 (published May 2025)
  • "Ownership" means whether people have one, not whether they use it every day
  • The Children and Families Agency figures come from a separate survey (fiscal 2025)

Summary

  • Smartphone ownership is 90.5% for households and 80.5% for individuals
  • 16.8% of people own a feature phone, and the figure is falling
  • People aged 6 to 12 and 80 and over are the least likely to own one. Almost all junior high students have their own smartphone
