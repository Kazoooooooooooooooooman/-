Local bookstores in Japan keep disappearing. A survey by the Japan Publishing Organization for Information Infrastructure Development (JPO) put the number of bookstores at 9,993 at the end of fiscal 2025, below 10,000 for the first time, according to The Yomiuri Shimbun. And according to the Japan Publishing Industry Foundation for Culture (JPIC), 493 municipalities (28.2%) had no bookstore at the end of November 2024. Every one of these numbers depends on what counts as a bookstore, so we have summarized them together with how they are counted.

  • According to JPIC, the share of municipalities with no bookstore was 27.9% at the end of August 2024 and 28.2% (493 municipalities) at the end of November

How the number of bookstores has changed

In JPO's survey, the number of bookstores fell from 14,658 in 2014 to 10,417 in fiscal 2024. At the end of fiscal 2025 (March 2026; Japan's fiscal year runs April–March) it was 9,993, down 424 in a year. According to The Yomiuri Shimbun, the peak was 24,237 in fiscal 1998, so today's number is just over 40% of that. The paper cites the spread of the internet, the growth of online retailers and a shrinking market for printed publications. How JPO counts bookstores

JPO's Bookstore Master Management Center gives each bookstore in Japan its own ID, collects information on new openings, renovations, changes of wholesale distributor and closures, and supplies this list to publishers. It was built as a directory for publishers' sales work.

There are 13,484 registrations in total, but only 10,927 of them are "bookstore shops." So even JPO's own numbers change depending on what is counted and when. In February 2026, JPO also explained that "independent bookstores" (shops that do not deal with a book wholesaler, or toritsugi) are registered only when they apply, so only some of them are included. The Yomiuri Shimbun likewise notes that the survey may not cover every bookstore because some independent shops are believed to be left out.

Municipalities with no bookstore

The Japan Publishing Industry Foundation for Culture (JPIC) tracks "bookstore-less municipalities," which have not a single bookstore. At the end of August 2024 they made up 27.9%, and municipalities with one bookstore or none made up 47.7%. At the end of November 2024, 493 municipalities (28.2%) had no bookstore.

Since February 2026, JPO has also published bookstore counts by prefecture and by city, ward and county. But JPO warns that an area showing "0" only means that no bookstore is registered there, not necessarily that there is no bookstore at all.

Reading the numbers

  • JPO's yearly bookstore counts (10,417, 9,993 and so on) were confirmed through press reports. JPO distributes its published data as Excel files
  • JPO's and JPIC's figures come from different organizations surveying for different purposes
  • JPO's count includes only some of the independent bookstores that do not deal with a book wholesaler

FAQ

How many bookstores are there in Japan?

According to press reports on a survey by the Japan Publishing Organization for Information Infrastructure Development (JPO), there were 9,993 at the end of fiscal 2025, below 10,000 for the first time.

How many municipalities have no bookstore?

According to the Japan Publishing Industry Foundation for Culture (JPIC), 493 municipalities, or 28.2% of the total, at the end of November 2024.
