In fiscal 2023, Japan's museums and art museums recorded 132.51 million visits. As of October 2024 there were 1,346 museums, the highest number since the survey began. Visitor numbers, which fell sharply during COVID, have almost returned to earlier levels. This summary is based on the Social Education Survey (fiscal 2024), which the Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology (MEXT) carries out about every three years.

The short version

  • There are 1,346 museums, 41 more than in the previous (2021) survey and the most on record
  • Museums recorded 132.51 million visits in fiscal 2023. By type, art museums had the most, at 37.07 million
  • Users per museum recovered from 52,611 in fiscal 2020 to 102,384 in fiscal 2023

How the number of museums has changed

In this survey, "museums" means registered museums under the Museum Act plus designated facilities (called "museum-equivalent facilities" up to fiscal 2021). In 2024 there were 972 registered museums and 374 designated facilities. Separately, there are 4,426 "museum-like facilities" that are neither registered museums nor designated facilities, and the survey counts them on their own. While community centers (kominkan) and youth education facilities are declining, museums and libraries are increasing.

History and art museums make up 70% of all museums. Art museums draw the most visits, about 28% of the total. Aquariums and zoos are few in number but each one draws many visitors. There are also open-air museums (17), botanical gardens (13) and combined zoo-botanical gardens (7). The visit figures are totals for the 1,318 museums that were open in fiscal 2023.

Before and after COVID

Users per museum fell from 116,131 in fiscal 2017 to 52,611 in fiscal 2020, the year hit by COVID, less than half. In fiscal 2023 the figure was 102,384, back to about the level of fiscal 2004–2010. It has not yet reached the fiscal 2017 level.

The same survey shows library use coming back too (library users in Japan).

Reading the numbers

  • The number of museums is as of October 1 of the survey year. Visitor and user figures are for the previous fiscal year (April to March; Japan's fiscal year runs April–March)
  • "Users per museum" adds participants in classes, lectures and other events to the number of visitors, then divides by the number of museums
  • Visits are counted each time: one person who goes several times is counted every time
  • Visits to the 4,426 museum-like facilities are not included in the figures in this article

FAQ

How many museums are there in Japan?

MEXT's Social Education Survey counted 1,346 as of October 2024. Separately, there are 4,426 museum-like facilities that are neither registered museums nor designated facilities.

How many people visit art museums?

In fiscal 2023, the 457 art museums that were open recorded 37.07 million visits. That is the most of any museum type.

Summary

  • Japan has a record 1,346 museums, which recorded 132.51 million visits in fiscal 2023
  • Art museums had 37.07 million visits, the most of any type
  • Users per museum fell by more than half during COVID and have almost returned to earlier levels
