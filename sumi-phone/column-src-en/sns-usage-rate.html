The most widely used social media service in Japan is LINE (Japan's most-used messaging app), with a usage rate of 92.9%. Next come YouTube at 81.5% and Instagram at 54.8%. We summarize usage rates for the main services by age and gender, based on the report (published June 2026) of a survey the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC) carried out in December 2025 with 1,800 people aged 13 to 79.

The short version

  • Usage rates across all ages: LINE 92.9%, YouTube 81.5%, Instagram 54.8%, X 44.7%, TikTok 36.7%, Facebook 27.0%
  • LINE is above 90% in every age group from teens to people in their 60s
  • Among teens, 67.9% use TikTok; among people in their 20s, 82.6% use Instagram and 73.4% use X

Ranking across all ages

The report notes that usage rates for Instagram and TikTok are rising.

The patterns split clearly by age.

  • Instagram and X are highest among people in their 20s
  • Facebook is highest among people in their 40s (38.9%), and 14.3% among teens

Points to keep in mind about these figures

  • In this survey, the "usage rate" is the share of respondents who said they use the service
  • It does not ask how often they use it
  • YouTube is a video service, but the survey asks about it as one of the social media and similar services

Summary

  • Usage rates: LINE 92.9%, YouTube 81.5%, Instagram 54.8%, X 44.7%, TikTok 36.7% (fiscal 2025)
  • Teens have high TikTok usage; people in their 20s have high Instagram and X usage
  • Instagram and TikTok are higher among women
