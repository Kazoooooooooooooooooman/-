More people than ever went to the stadium. Total attendance for the 2025 season of the J.League (Japan's professional soccer league) was 13,503,210, passing 13 million for the first time. That is up 8% from 12.54 million the year before. The average per J1 match was 21,246. This summary is based on figures released by the J.League.

The short version

  • Total attendance was 13,503,210, a record for the second year in a row
  • Records were also set for the opening round and for a single day
  • More people are choosing to watch matches in person

At the stadium, not on a screen

Matches can be watched on streaming services, yet the number of people going to stadiums keeps growing. In the same year, concerts and movie theaters also hit record highs (the live entertainment market, movie theater attendance). People are once again choosing to share the same moment with a crowd.

Things to keep in mind about the figures

  • These are J.League figures that combine J1, J2, J3 and other competitions
  • Attendance counts visits, not individual people. If one person goes to several matches, each visit is counted
