On a weekday, high school students in Japan use the internet for about 6 hours 44 minutes on average. That is almost as long as they spend at school, and about 4 hours of it is on smartphones alone. We have pulled together the figures by age and gender from the report of a survey that Japan's Children and Families Agency carried out in November and December 2025 (published in March 2026).

The short version

  • High school students spend an average of 404.0 minutes (about 6 hours 44 minutes) online on a weekday
  • Time spent online on a smartphone averages 242.8 minutes (about 4 hours)
  • 63.3% of high school students are online for 5 hours or more. The longest is boys aged 17, at 443.2 minutes

About 6 hours 44 minutes online on a weekday

In the Children and Families Agency's "Survey on Internet Use Among Young People," high school students who use the internet (972 respondents) were online for an average of 404.0 minutes on a weekday. That is about 1 hour 20 minutes longer than junior high school students (324.3 minutes).

This total covers seven kinds of devices, including smartphones, tablets handed out by schools, game consoles and TVs, and includes use for studying. 86.0% of high school students were online for "3 hours or more," and 63.3% for "5 hours or more."

By age and gender: boys aged 17 spend the longest

Broken down by age and gender, boys aged 17 spent the longest online, at 443.2 minutes (about 7 hours 23 minutes).

Across all high school students, boys averaged 403.8 minutes and girls 404.1 minutes, so there is almost no difference. Time by purpose (the average among students who use the internet for each purpose) was as follows.

What they use smartphones for: messaging, video, music

97.8% of high school students who use the internet go online with a smartphone, and their smartphone time averaged 242.8 minutes. Here is what they use it for, from most to least common.

  1. Messaging, such as social media and email (93.2%)
  2. Watching videos (91.2%)
  3. Listening to music (89.2%)
  4. Searching and looking things up (87.9%)
  5. Games (75.1%)
  6. Maps (64.2%)

42.8% of high school students use generative AI (such as ChatGPT) on their smartphones, a big jump from junior high school students (27.0%).

Fewer than half of families have rules

44.3% of high school students said their family has rules about how they use the internet, far fewer than elementary (83.8%) or junior high school students (74.5%). For phone use during exam season, see phone tips for students preparing for entrance exams.

Things to keep in mind about the numbers

  • These are averages for high school students who use the internet, and they include use for studying
  • The times are self-reported, so they may differ from actual use
