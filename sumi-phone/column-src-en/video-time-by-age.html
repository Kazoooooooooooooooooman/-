On an average weekday, people of all ages spend 54.5 minutes a day watching YouTube and other videos. That is the longest of any use of the internet, longer than social media (36.2 minutes). Teens spent 127.2 minutes on weekdays and 166.1 minutes (about 2 hours 46 minutes) on days off. This summary, by age and gender, is based on the report (published June 2026) of a survey the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC) carried out in December 2025.

The short version

  • Video-sharing services (YouTube and similar): 54.5 minutes on weekdays and 73.1 minutes on days off, across all ages
  • Teens: 127.2 minutes on weekdays and 166.1 minutes on days off. People in their 20s also spend 151.0 minutes on days off
  • Men watch longer. Teenage boys spend 199.5 minutes (about 3 hours 20 minutes) on days off

By age: over 2.5 hours on days off for teens and people in their 20s

In the survey by MIC's Institute for Information and Communications Policy, 1,800 people aged 13 to 79 recorded what they did each day in 15-minute blocks, and time was calculated for each use of the internet. Things to keep in mind about the numbers

  • These averages include people who do not use the service, counted as 0 minutes
  • They include viewing on devices other than smartphones (computers, devices connected to a TV and so on)
  • How people recorded watching while doing something else varies from person to person

Summary

  • Watching YouTube and other videos: 54.5 minutes on weekdays and 73.1 minutes on days off, across all ages (fiscal 2025)

  • Men watch longer. Teenage boys spend 199.5 minutes on days off
  • On weekdays, it is the longest of any use of the internet across all ages
