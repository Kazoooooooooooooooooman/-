Junior high school students in Japan (roughly ages 12 to 15) use the internet for an average of about 5 hours 24 minutes on a weekday. About 3 hours of that is on smartphones alone. We summarize time by gender and what students use it for, based on the report of a survey the Children and Families Agency carried out in November and December 2025 (published March 2026).

The short version

  • Junior high students use the internet for an average of 324.3 minutes (about 5 hours 24 minutes) on weekdays
  • Time online on a smartphone averages 180.3 minutes (about 3 hours)
  • 78.1% of junior high students are online for 3 hours or more, and 48.0% for 5 hours or more

About 5 hours 24 minutes online on weekdays

In the Children and Families Agency's "Survey on the Internet Usage Environment of Young People", junior high students who use the internet (1,199 people) averaged 324.3 minutes online on weekdays. That is about an hour and a half longer than elementary school students aged 10 and over (233.6 minutes).

This time is the total across seven kinds of devices, including smartphones, school-issued tablets, game consoles and TVs, and it includes use for study. 78.1% of junior high students are online for "3 hours or more" and 48.0% for "5 hours or more", so almost one in two goes over 5 hours.

The time for each use is the average among junior high students who use the internet for that purpose. Boys spend longer on hobbies and entertainment, and girls spend a little longer messaging.

On smartphones, messaging such as social media comes first

85.5% of junior high students who use the internet go online on a smartphone, and their average smartphone time was 180.3 minutes. Their uses on smartphones, from most to least common, are:

  1. Messaging such as social media and email (90.0%)
  2. Watching videos (86.6%)
  3. Searching and looking things up (86.0%)
  4. Listening to music (80.1%)
  5. Games (72.8%)

Among elementary school students, "videos" came first; among junior high students, "messaging" overtakes it. 27.0% of junior high students use generative AI (such as ChatGPT) on their smartphones, more than three times the rate for elementary school students (8.2%).

Three in four households have rules

74.5% of junior high students said their family has set rules for how they use the internet. That is lower than for elementary school students (83.8%) and higher than for high school students (44.3%). For phone use before tests, see also how to stop picking up your phone while studying.

Points to keep in mind about these figures

  • These are averages for junior high students who use the internet, and they include studying on school tablets
  • The times are self-reported, so they may differ from actual time

Summary

  • Junior high students are online for an average of about 5 hours 24 minutes on weekdays, about 3 hours of it on smartphones (fiscal 2025)
  • 48.0% of junior high students are online for 5 hours or more
  • The most common smartphone use is messaging such as social media (90.0%)
  • 27.0% of junior high students use generative AI on their smartphones
