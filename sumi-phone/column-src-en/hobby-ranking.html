Among people aged 10 and over, the most common hobby or pastime over the past year was "listening to music on CDs, smartphones and so on" (53.5%). The top of the list is filled with things you can do at home with a screen or earphones. Meanwhile, hobbies that take you out of the house, such as karaoke and amusement parks, fell sharply from five years earlier. This summary is based on the Survey on Time Use and Leisure Activities by the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications.

The short version

  • No. 1: listening to music, 53.5%. No. 2: watching movies (not at a cinema), 52.7%. No. 3: games, 42.9%
  • Compared with five years earlier, games (+7.1 points) and listening to music (+4.5 points), among others, went up
  • Karaoke went from 30.7% to 13.5%, amusement parks, zoos, botanical gardens and aquariums from 33.8% to 19.0%, and travel and outings from 73.5% to 49.5%

The hobby ranking (2021)

The participation rate is the share of people aged 10 and over who did the activity in the past year (October 2020 to October 2021). Doing it even once counts.

Hobbies you can do at home went up, while hobbies that take you out went down across the board. The survey period included two states of emergency, so the COVID-19 pandemic is thought to have had a big effect.

Fewer than half went traveling or on outings

49.5% of people went traveling or on an outing in the past year, down 24.0 points from five years earlier. Up to 2011, the survey also counted things like business trips, so be careful when comparing. We have summarized what travel costs in the average cost of domestic travel.

Have one hobby that takes you outside

  • Start with something you can do alone: walks, photography, sento public baths, browsing secondhand bookstores and so on (things to enjoy outside on your own)
  • Hobbies that get you moving: walking went up compared with five years earlier (the sports ranking)

Summary

  • The top hobby is listening to music (53.5%). The top spots go to things you can do at home
  • Games rose to 42.9%, while karaoke fell to 13.5%
  • 49.5% of people went traveling or on outings, a big drop from five years earlier
