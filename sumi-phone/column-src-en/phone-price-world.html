People have long said that "mobile phone plans in Japan are expensive". What about now? In a survey by the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC) comparing prices in six cities, using 20 GB a month with the carrier that has the largest market share cost 2,973 yen in Tokyo. It was 5,713 yen in New York and 6,731 yen in Seoul. For every usage pattern, Tokyo lands around the middle of the six cities. We have put together prices by data allowance.

How the comparison works

  • For each city, it takes the carrier with the largest market share (its main brand) and compares the cheapest general consumer plan, available to new customers, that fits each usage pattern
  • The usage patterns are modeled on how people in Japan actually use their phones: 44 minutes of calls and 60 messages a month
  • Overseas prices are converted to yen using OECD purchasing power parities (2024), not exchange rates
  • Prices are the published rates as of March 12–19, 2025

FAQ

Are mobile phone plans in Japan expensive by world standards?

In MIC's six-city comparison (as of March 2025), Tokyo's prices were in the middle for every usage pattern. They are cheaper than Seoul and New York, and more expensive than Paris and London.

Which city is the cheapest?

For 20 GB, Paris at 2,402 yen. For unlimited plans, London is the cheapest at 4,073 yen.

Which carrier do the Tokyo figures come from?

They are the prices of the main brand of the carrier with the largest market share in Tokyo. The report's summary does not name the company; it refers to it as "the operator with the largest share".

Summary

  • For 20 GB a month, Tokyo costs 2,973 yen, Seoul 6,731 yen and Paris 2,402 yen
  • Tokyo is in the middle of the six cities for every usage pattern
  • The comparison uses purchasing power parities to convert to yen, as of March 2025
