In 2025, Japanese residents spent an average of 48,424 yen per person on each domestic trip. Overnight trips cost 72,412 yen and day trips 20,000 yen. That is about 30% more than in 2019, before COVID (37,355 yen). This summary is based on a survey by the Japan Tourism Agency.

The short version

  • Average spending per person per domestic trip: 48,424 yen (72,412 yen overnight, 20,000 yen for a day trip)
  • Total trips: 553.13 million (299.97 million overnight, 253.16 million day trips)
  • Total trips have not yet returned to the 2019 level (587.10 million)

Cost per trip over time

Overnight trips rose from 55,054 yen in 2019 to 72,412 yen, an increase of about 17,000 yen. Spending includes transportation, accommodation, food and drink, shopping, admission fees and so on.

How many trips people take

The number of trips has not yet returned to pre-COVID levels. But because each trip costs more, the total amount spent on domestic travel (26.7845 trillion yen) was the highest since 2016.

Ways to go out for less

  • Make it a day trip: On average, it costs less than a third of an overnight trip
  • Turn somewhere nearby into a "trip": Even a town an hour away by train can show you unfamiliar sights if you put your phone away and walk (see things to enjoy outside on your own)
  • Make a sento or sauna your destination: For a few hundred yen, a public bath gives you a taste of being away (see your first sento and sauna)

Notes on the survey

  • "Total trips" counts one person who travels twice as two. It is not the number of people who traveled
  • Business trips and visits home to family are also counted as travel

Summary

  • The average domestic trip costs 48,424 yen
  • An overnight trip costs 72,412 yen, about 30% more than in 2019
  • The number of trips has not returned to pre-COVID levels
