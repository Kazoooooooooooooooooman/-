Even now that we can watch videos at home, more people are going to the cinema. In 2025, Japanese cinemas recorded 188.75 million admissions, up 30.7% from the year before. That is the second-highest figure ever, after 2019. Box office revenue was 274.4 billion yen, a record high. This summary is based on figures from the Motion Picture Producers Association of Japan.

Why did people come back to the cinema?

The main reason is that several big hits came out in the same year. Unlike streaming at home, watching on a big screen with big sound, together with other people in the audience, is an experience many people seem to value. Concerts and stage shows have also hit record highs (the live entertainment market in 2025).

Reading these numbers

  • These figures come from the "Overview of the Japanese Film Industry," which the Motion Picture Producers Association of Japan publishes every January
  • We checked them through news reports and industry media this time, and the figures for each film are as of the announcement
