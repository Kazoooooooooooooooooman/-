On January 25, 2024, Spain's State School Council (Consejo Escolar del Estado), a national body for education, unanimously approved recommendations on phones in schools: zero use in early childhood and primary education, and phones kept switched off during the school day in secondary school. But this is a national "recommendation". The actual rules are made by Spain's regions (autonomous communities), which are responsible for education. This summary is based on announcements from Spain's Ministry of Education and on regional official gazettes.

The short version

  • What the national level adopted is a recommendation, not a law: zero use up to primary school, phones off in secondary school as the basic rule
  • The national government sets out a "minimum consensus", and each region then decides under its own powers
  • Madrid, Galicia, Castilla-La Mancha and others restrict phones in schools through regional rules

The national steps: December 2023 to January 2024

DateWhat happened
December 13, 2023At a meeting of national and regional education officials (the Sectoral Conference on Education), the education minister presented the national proposal: "0 minutes" of use in primary school, and use in secondary school only under teacher supervision. She announced talks with the regions in January
January 25, 2024The standing committee of the State School Council unanimously approved recommendations based on the national proposal
January 31, 2024The education minister held a working meeting with representatives of the regions

The State School Council is a national body where representatives of the school community, such as teachers, parents and students, sit together. The Ministry of Education said these recommendations would be the basis for its instructions.

What the recommendations say

StageRecommendation (January 25, 2024)
Early childhood and primaryZero phone use
Secondary (compulsory secondary education)Phones stay switched off during the school day. They can be used when a teacher considers it necessary for a specific learning activity
All stagesExceptions for health, safety or special needs

At the January 31 meeting, the ministry's proposal to the regions was zero use in early childhood and primary education, and, in secondary school, upper secondary (bachillerato) and vocational training, use limited to learning activities when the teacher considers it appropriate. Some regions asked that schools be allowed to set their own rules for the stages after compulsory education.

The state recommends, the regions decide

The January 31 meeting was a "working meeting", not a formal decision-making session. The education minister said the goal was a "minimum consensus", after which each region would issue its own recommendations or instructions under its own powers. In other words, Spain did not pass a single nationwide law banning phones.

According to the Ministry of Education, as of January 31, 2024, seven territories (including Ceuta and Melilla) had started their own rules after the December meeting, and four already had some kind of rule before that. The rest were still discussing the issue with their school councils and school communities.

Regional rules: Madrid, Galicia and Castilla-La Mancha

RegionRuleWhat it says
MadridDecree 60/2020; Decree 64/2025 (July 23, 2025)The 2020 decree banned phone use during the school day (use as a teaching tool and some other uses allowed). The 2025 decree changed this to: "phone use is not allowed for the whole school day", with use only in cases of necessity and exceptional circumstances, following the school's rules
GaliciaProtocol (published January 4, 2024)Use had been restricted in class since 2015. From the return after the Christmas holidays, the restriction was extended to the whole school day, including breaks, arriving and leaving, lunch and extracurricular activities
Castilla-La ManchaRegional education orders (Orden 140/2024 for the 2024–25 school year, Orden 104/2026 for 2026–27)Early childhood and primary: as a general rule, students do not bring phones to school. Compulsory secondary: as a general rule, no use during the school day. Upper secondary and vocational training: as a general rule, no use inside the classroom
  • Madrid: the 2025 decree restricts not only phones but also tablets and computers. From the 2025–26 school year, early childhood and primary schools stopped one-device-per-student use, and shared use is capped at one to two hours a week depending on the grade. The regional government says this covers more than 550,000 students
  • Galicia: phones can be used when there is a justified reason and the school management authorizes it, or for learning under teacher supervision. Stages such as higher vocational training and adult education can choose not to apply the break-time rule
  • Castilla-La Mancha: in compulsory secondary school, phones can still be used for learning under teacher supervision. The 2026–27 order adds that this applies "if the teaching team so decides". Each school sets the detailed rules in its own regulations

FAQ

Are phones banned in Spanish schools by law?

There is no single national law that bans them everywhere. What the State School Council approved in January 2024 is a recommendation, and the actual rules are made region by region. The Community of Madrid's regional decree says phone use is not allowed for the whole school day.
