165min

The daily average.
The highest it has ever been.

Source: Hakuhodo Institute of Media Environment, Media Teiten Survey 2025 (Tokyo, ages 15–69)

That’s about 42 days a year.

165 minutes × 365 days is about 1,000 hours.

I was in those hours too.

All that’s left is emptiness and a tired brain.
And a strange sense of satisfaction.

and turned them off every time.

Points

Time with your phone down
turns into points.

You earn points for every hour you keep Soto Phone’s screen off. Spend them at partner shops nearby.

  1. 01Pick your phone-free timeDinner, before bed, and so on.
  2. 02Keep the screen offMake it and earn +10 pt an hour
  3. 03Pick a place on the mapShops picked by pros that take your points.
  4. 04Spend it theree.g. a bathhouse ticket: −100 pt

1 hour at dinner × 10 days = 100 pt = 1 bathhouse visit

Why do I get points?

Partner shops pay for them. For a shop, it replaces advertising: they pay Soto only when a customer actually walks in.

Point numbers are examples. Partners and rewards are still being prepared.

Letter

Why I'm building this.

I've never built a phone.
But I know what it's like to be controlled by one.

I'd look at my screen, and the day would be gone. I set limits to stop, and turned them off myself. Willpower didn't work.

I think a lot of people feel the same way. So I decided to build a phone that makes you want to go outside, without relying on willpower.

I can't build it alone. I'll share the road to launch honestly, right here. I'd be glad if you followed along.

Kazuma Sasaki, Founder, Soto Phone
