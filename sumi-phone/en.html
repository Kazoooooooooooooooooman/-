How it began

Somewhere along the way,
my reasons to go outside disappeared.

Social media is fun. It makes me laugh.

I’m not missing anything.
I feel full every day, so why not?
I can talk to friends online. It’s the best.

…right?

There’s no need to leave the house.
Nobody tells me what to do,
and I can do what I like all day.

Nothing bad happens. It’s easy.

I’m not going to change anyway.
AI can do everything,
so I don’t need to learn anything.

But deep down,
part of me still wants
to become someone.

Part of me wants to spend
the time I give to social media and games
on something that matters.

I want to change this,
even if it’s little by little.

I want to use my time,
learn all sorts of things,
and try something new.

But I can’t do it anyway.
I’ve always run away,
it would take a lifetime,
and there’s no guarantee.

I’ll just stay as I am.

Wait a second.

Design

A small screen
in black and white.

Small and slim, with touch controls and no physical keyboard. The matte OLED screen stays black and white, and color returns only when you open your photos.

Planned specs, still in development. Size and parts will be decided through prototyping.
