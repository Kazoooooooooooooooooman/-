Soto Phone — Product Requirements (v0.2)

Soto Phone (working name) · Prepared for ODM / design-house discussions, Shenzhen, November 2026 · Contact: Kazuma Sasaki, Founder
Items marked TBD are not decided yet. We welcome your recommendations on every item.

1. Product summary

A compact Android phone with a square screen, sold in Japan. It is designed as a second phone: people carry it when they want to step away from their main smartphone and spend time outside. It has no social media, no games and no app store. It is sold together with a monthly eSIM plan.

  • Target users: adults and students in Japan who want less screen time.
  • Positioning: a well-made, calm object, not a rugged or budget phone.
  • Expected first order: 1,000–3,000 units (TBD, depends on your MOQ and price).

2. Hardware

DisplaySquare (1:1) touchscreen, about 3.9–4.0 inch diagonal, about 720 × 720 or higher. IPS LCD or AMOLED (TBD, please propose). Thin bezels.
Body sizeAbout 76–80 mm wide and tall, about 8.5–10 mm thick (TBD; final size follows the panel and battery). Rounded corners.
Rear cameraOne fixed camera built into the back (no flip or rotating camera, no pop-up module). About 13 MP, autofocus, with LED flash. The camera should not stick out more than about 1 mm if possible.
Front cameraNot required (TBD: please tell us the cost difference).
ChipsetMediaTek, mid-range 4G (for example Helio G-series). Please propose the platform you already have a board for.
Memory4 GB RAM + 64 GB storage. Please also quote 4 GB + 128 GB.
Battery2,500 mAh or more. USB-C charging. Battery must carry a printed capacity label.
Cellular4G LTE, Japanese bands B1 / B3 / B8 / B18 / B19 / B26 / B28 (B41 preferred). VoLTE on docomo, au, SoftBank and Rakuten Mobile.
SIMeSIM is required. One physical nano-SIM slot is optional (TBD).
WirelessWi-Fi 2.4/5 GHz, Bluetooth 5.x, GPS (+ QZSS/Michibiki for Japan if possible), NFC.
SensorsProximity, ambient light, accelerometer. Fingerprint is optional (TBD).
ButtonsPower, volume up, volume down. Position is flexible; please place them where your board design works best.
AudioEarpiece, loudspeaker, microphone. Headphone jack not required.
VibrationLinear vibration motor preferred.

4. Software (most important)

We will build, maintain and update our own operating system for several years. We need full control of the software.

OSAOSP (Android Open Source Project). No GMS is needed. Please state the Android version.
Source codeFull BSP source code: kernel, drivers, HAL, vendor modules, and a working build environment, so that our engineers can build complete system images ourselves. Please state any license or NDA required (for example with MediaTek) and the cost.
Signing keysWe must hold the platform and release signing keys, or generate our own. Our launcher and system apps are signed with the platform key.
Bootloader & flashingBootloader that can be unlocked for development units. Flashing tool and procedure (for example SP Flash Tool), so that we can re-flash devices at any time.
UpdatesOTA update system that we can operate ourselves. Security patches for at least 3 years: please tell us who prepares them and the cost.
Pre-installed appsOnly our own apps: launcher, phone, SMS, camera, maps, transit, settings. No other pre-installed apps, no ads.
Notifications without GMSPlease share your experience with push notifications (for example LINE) on AOSP devices without GMS, such as microG or other methods.

5. Certification and compliance

  • Japan radio certification (MIC / Giteki) and telecom terminal approval (JATE). Please state cost, time and who applies.
  • Battery safety certifications needed for shipping (for example UN38.3).
  • RoHS.

6. Samples and schedule

  • Stage 1: appearance (ID) samples to confirm size and feel.
  • Stage 2: working engineering samples (EVT/DVT) with our software.
  • Stage 3: small pilot batch of about 100 units for real users, if possible.
  • Stage 4: mass production.

Please propose a timeline for each stage.

7. Next milestone: one working engineering handset

Before any custom housing or tooling, we want one working engineering unit: your existing Android reference board with the square 3.9–4.0 inch panel and touch controller connected, plus the software build package and flashing tools. We will accept the unit when these tests pass:

A. We control the softwareOur engineers build AOSP from your source package on a clean machine, flash the handset with your tool, and install an OTA update signed with our own keys.
B. It works as a phoneActivate an eSIM and test incoming and outgoing calls (VoLTE), SMS and LTE data. Note: in Japan we can only transmit with a Giteki-certified unit, so please tell us how this test can be done legally (for example a certified sample, or testing in China on a local network).
C. It fitsA dimensioned layout of the panel, a 2,500 mAh battery and the fixed rear camera, showing the minimum body thickness, before we pay for enclosure tooling.

Please quote the cost and lead time of this engineering unit separately.
